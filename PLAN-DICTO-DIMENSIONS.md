# DICTO by dimension (before step D3)

DICTO, 2026-10-09: "apps themselves shouldn't start with dimensions; a first generic choice lets you
select what you want dimension-wise, then it takes you inside the app you selected through the
dimension choice. Reason before code."

## Decisions (DICTO, 2026-10-09)

- **Dimension first.** DICTO opens on a choice of dimension (1D+ … 6D), not on an app. Inside a
  dimension, every app's offering is listed; choosing one enters that app's space (its colours, its
  tools).
- **Shared worlds once, in Kaleidohedra.** Worlds both Kaleidohedra and Rhombiverse have (Nets, EKP,
  Sunstar Lattice, Stella–Jewel Lattice, Shells, Golden Rhombohedra) are listed once and open in
  Kaleidohedra's space, where the Shear can move them too.
- **Grouped by kind**, each entry tagged with its app's colour (3D: Lattices · Worlds · Shapes).
- **Doors**: all three addresses land on the same dimension choice; the door sets the colour and lists
  its own app's entries first.
- **Before D3**, so Polyhedraverse's building lands in the new structure.

## Proposed screens (to confirm before code)

1. **DICTO**: the dimension cards, 1D+ to 6D, each with its turning preview and a line saying what is
   in it and from which apps (small coloured app tags).
2. **A dimension**, e.g. 3D+: sections by kind; each entry a turning wireframe with an app tag:
   - *Lattices*: Kaleidohedra's and Rhombiverse's lattices. Open question: Kaleidohedra's lattices
     are Rhombiverse's lattices that can also be sheared; list each lattice once (opening in
     Kaleidohedra, the Shear available) or both?
   - *Worlds*: EKP, Studies, Stella–Jewel, Sunstar, Targets, Shells, Golden.
   - *Shapes*: Polyhedraverse's 13 families (then a family's shapes, as now).
3. **Entering**: the app's space, its colours and tools, as now.

## Settled (DICTO, 2026-10-09)

- **Principle: never land in one app with little choice.** The dimension screen always shows every
  app's entries; nothing hides another app's work unless you ask it to.
- **No repeats** (DICTO, later the same day, replacing "once per app"): each dimension is divided into
  app blocks in their own colours, the door's app first; a lattice or piece already listed by an
  earlier block is not listed again.
- **App buttons stay, as a filter**: optional, to show one app's entries only; off by default.
- **2D+: one list** (Nets, the tilings, Kaleidoscope).
