Background for you (not for pasting)

Facts you could put in your own words: Samson (#51, The Delian Bond) is the cube with four alternate corners cut off at depth 2^(-1/3) (A270714) along the edges. Under the Star Cores core rule (#78) its first core is a truncated tetrahedron, and every later core is the same shape mirrored and scaled by t/3. In that truncated tetrahedron, the long side of each hexagon divided by the side of each triangle is this constant. Exact derivation: a cut at depth a gives the ratio 2a/(3(1 - a)); a = 2^(-1/3) gives 2/(3(2^(1/3) - 1)).
