Comments: Samson (#51, The Delian Bond) is the name for a unit cube with four alternate corners cut off at depth 2^(-1/3) (A270714) along the edges. In accordance with the Star Cores core rule its initial core is a truncated tetrahedron, and each and every later core is the same shape mirrored and scaled down. The long side of each hexagon (of that truncated tetrahedron) divided by the side of each triangle is this constant. A cut at depth a gives the ratio 2a/(3(1 - a)); a = 2^(-1/3) gives 2/(3(2^(1/3) - 1)).
