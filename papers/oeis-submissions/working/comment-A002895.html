Background for you (not for pasting)

What to say, in your own words: 1. These numbers also count the closed 2n-step walks from any atom of lonsdaleite and of the tetrahedral network of every close-packed stacking (4H, 6H, 9R and so on, periodic or not), even though the neighbor shells of these atoms differ. 2. For periodic stackings this was known (Thorpe 1972; Nagle 1966 for ice). New in #102 (Lemma 2) is that it holds at every single atom, including aperiodic stackings. Words to check in your text: "lonsdaleite"; "polytypes" (not polytopes); "close-packed".
