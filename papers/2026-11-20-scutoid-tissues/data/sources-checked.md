# Sources checked for Theorem 1 (swaps = corners - 2 x cells), 20 November 2026

Question: does any source state the counts of a generic plane sweep of a simple tessellation with convex cells (births = deaths =
cells, swaps = corners - 2 x cells, swaps per birth = <F>/2 - 3)? Free sources only; nothing bought.

## Read (full text extracted and searched, or the relevant sections read)

- Gusakova, Kabluchko, Thäle, Sectional Voronoi tessellations: characterization and high-dimensional limits, arXiv:2301.03297 (2023).
  Searched for sweep, moving, time, dynamic, topolog, neighbo, exchange, transition: no moving sections or event counts. Credits the
  "few mean values" of sectional Poisson-Voronoi tessellations to Chiu, van de Weygaert, Stoyan 1996 [6] and Miles 1984 [20].
- De Leo, Topology of plane sections of periodic polyhedra with an application to the truncated octahedron, arXiv:math/0502219 (2005).
  Banchoff's critical points of height functions on periodic polyhedral surfaces; minima/maxima index +1, saddles -1, index sum =
  Euler characteristic per period. A surface, not a cell complex; no counts of events in a tiling of cells.
- Redenbach, Jung, Random tessellations: an overview of models, arXiv:2501.05166 (2025). Definitions of face-to-face and normal
  tessellations; mean value relations for normal tessellations in R^3 in terms of gamma_3, gamma_0, L_V, S_V (citing Mecke; Radecke).
  No sweep.
- Mughal, Cox, Weaire, Burke, Hutzler, arXiv:1809.08421 (Phil. Mag. Lett. 98, 2018). Full text (about 2000 words): scutoids in a
  froth between curved plates; T1 vocabulary; no count of swaps per birth.
- Gómez-Gálvez et al., Nat. Commun. 9 (2018) 2960 (open access copy). Searched for Euler, birth, appear, intercalat, T1, Voronoi:
  Voronoi models of apical and basal surfaces; apico-basal intercalations; no count of swaps per birth.
- Gómez-Gálvez et al., A quantitative biophysical principle to explain the 3D cellular connectivity in curved epithelia (author
  manuscript, UCL Discovery eprint 10155692). Passages on Euler's formula (<n> = 6 on a surface) and the average number of apico-basal
  intercalations per cell <i>, measured on Voronoi tubes and tissues; not related to the number of walls; no births.
- Mason, Lazar, MacPherson, Srolovitz, arXiv:1507.03379 (Phys. Rev. E 92, 063308, 2015). Steady-state <F> = 13.766 faces per grain;
  cross-sections studied as static statistics.
- Ferraro, Zaninetti, arXiv:1110.2337. Distributions of lengths and areas of plane sections of 3D Voronoi tessellations; no events.

## Conclusion

No source read states the sweep counts of Theorem 1 or swaps per birth = <F>/2 - 3. Ingredients are classical (corner index in a
sweep, McMullen's line shellings and #104; unique lowest vertex of a linear function on a convex polytope; Euler's formula for simple
polytopes; Banchoff's critical points). Claim: the combination, "not found in the sources checked".
