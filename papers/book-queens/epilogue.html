Epilogue. What has been shown

Conclusion

The book set out to see two solids of four-dimensional space, completely and exactly, and to find what ties them to the solids of our own space. The lead-up gave the pieces: three small pieces close the gaps between icosahedra (Chapter 1); of Aristotle's five pyramids only the cube's, and the tetrahedron's with the octahedron's, fill space (Chapter 2); face to face the five keep to their own solids, and glued together DICTO's pieces make no new deltahedron in the range searched (Chapter 3). The shadows then gave the new shapes that gluing could not: variants of the convex deltahedra that join differently, and deltahedra rebuilt with every edge equal (Chapter 4).

The White Queen has been mapped completely: 62 slice patterns in her four main directions, every one exact, twelve made only of triangles; four shadows, each resolving into an exact front half and back half; and her 600 cells in fifteen shells round one corner, the innermost a core of twenty Cleo-Icos, the icosahedron's pyramid (Chapter 5). Alicia Boole Stott's series of 1900 is the first part of this atlas, and its cell-first slices at the corner layers are hers. The Red Queen, built exactly from the White Queen, mirrors her by polarity, shadow for middle slice, and holds the dodecahedron's pyramid at her core. Aristotle's five pyramids are the cores of the four-dimensional regular solids, each with exactly its proportions, in polar pairs (Chapter 6). The pyramid Aristotle said fills space is, seen from four dimensions, the core of a regular solid.

Open problems

(1) Which tilings of space by the tetrahedron's and the octahedron's pyramids exist besides the octet cut up and its rearrangements (Chapter 2)? (2) Do gluings of five or more pieces with the regular solids, or six or more of DICTO's pieces alone, give a new deltahedron, and do icosahedra with Antonius fill space (Chapter 3)? (3) Which of the new deltahedra of Chapter 4 tile space, alone or with others? (4) The atlases of Chapters 5 and 6 cover the four main directions; the slices in other directions are not mapped.
