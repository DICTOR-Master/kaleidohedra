The point of the paper

The Śrī Yantra's nine triangles must meet three at a time at 18 points. It has been drawn by eye and solved by numbers; this paper gives exact yantras, a rational one, and the DICTO Yantra built on solids, flat and in three dimensions.

Main results

Proved exactly

  1. Theorem 1 (the quadratic family). Choose the two great triangles' slopes and two base depths as fractions: every corner and every meeting point of the yantra then lies in one quadratic field, and all 18 meetings hold exactly.
  2. Theorem 2 (a rational Śrī Yantra). An explicit yantra with every coordinate a fraction (depths 248/3611 … 8/9), checked by two independent programs.
  3. Proposition 3 (the 43). The 43 triangles are exactly the pieces lying inside an odd number of the nine triangles (14, 10, 10, 8, 1 ring by ring); with them come 18 marmas and 24 sandhis.
  4. Proposition 4 (in three dimensions). Spin each triangle into a cone about the axis: the flat yantra is exactly the axial section of nine nested cones, and the 18 marmas become 9 circles where two cones and a level disc meet. Still tetrahedra with level bases show the yantra from three positions, narrowed to cos 30°, every meeting kept.

Computed to 60–80 digits (exact forms stated as open)

  1. DICTO's rhombus. The long sides of the two great triangles form an upright rhombus (DICTO); it is a true rhombus only when the great triangles are equal, and then every yantra tried has 45 pieces and 28 sandhis, not 43 and 24.
  2. The DICTO Yantra on the Cleo-Octangula: a Cleo-Icos above (slope 2/φ²), the stella octangula's tetrahedron below (1/√2), both small triangles on the circle: 43, 18, 24.
  3. The perfect stella yantra (DICTO's choice): the stella exact below, both small triangles on the circle and the bindu centred, all traditional counts. Its upper slope 0.695886 belongs to no regular 4D, Platonic or dataset solid: it is the yantra's own number.
  4. The viewpoint (DICTO). The perspectives that keep the circle round (Klein translations along the axis) keep every meeting, crossing, level base and corner on the circle, and keep the product of the two slopes; seen from the one that centres the bindu, the DICTO Yantra meets every condition at once (43, 18, 24, gnomon): the perfect yantra is the Cleo-Octangula in perspective.
  5. The gnomon. With the bindu centred, the yantra holds an exact copy of itself in its bindu: the yantras nest without end (ratio 0.0631 on the Cleo-Octangula, 0.0563 on the perfect stella yantra).
