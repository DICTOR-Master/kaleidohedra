About. DICTO is a geometric sculptor in Osaka whose work grows out of building with recycled PET and from three open apps on one shared geometry, Kaleidohedra, Rhombiverse and Polyhedraverse. This record gathers DICTO's published geometry under one DOI. Each work below has its own DICTO DOI, always pointing to its latest version; the numbers are those of DICTO's findings on dictospheres.com. Version 13 adds #38, Universal PiTet: The DICTO PiTet Proposition; Version 12 added #37, The DICTO Ogdoecontahedron on the Octet Truss; Version 11 added #36, Golden Bodhi Pyrochlore; Version 10 corrected the paper count and named the seven findings published with the app; Version 9 added #34a, The DICTO Hecatogdoecontahedron, and #31a, The DICTO OCD Stella (the third and fourth short introductions, one per new solid); Version 8 added #35b, The DICTO Palintriacontahedron (the second); Version 7 added #35a, The DICTO Metaxyhexecontahedron (the first); Version 6 added #35, Vajrayana; Version 5 added #34, Progenitors; Version 4 lists the Version 2 updates of #28, #31, #32 and #33; Version 3 added Double Vision (#33); Version 2 added the twelve papers published or given their own DOI since Version 1 (14 October 2026).

#38Universal PiTet: The DICTO PiTet Proposition (2026)From the Cleo star outward in spherical shells: an endless, self-polar cycle of four solids, icosidodecahedron, rhombic triacontahedron, DICTO GTI and DICTO FPD, closing exactly 10/φ⁴ larger each turn, with exact shell pieces, Monkey Staff B, five-sided Vajras and hexagonal Cleos.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23463305
#37The DICTO Ogdoecontahedron on the Octet Truss (2026)Exact space-filling kits with the OG in the octahedra of the octet truss: golden-section keys; an exact Penrose link between facing five-fold stars, with three Penrose cores; golden envelopes filling exactly 10/φ⁶ of space, with a two-shape kit and a tetartoid core; and one-piece glue with exact OG imprints, keyed with the OG's own kites so that twelve lock round one OG.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23457016
#36Golden Bodhi Pyrochlore (2026)The gap of the Ogdoecontahedron's bcc packing, exact: 72 cone pieces, limbs crossing in golden rhombi, the canonical golden cut that shatters, the GBPC that every golden fill needs, and no face-to-face tiling of what remains by golden rhombohedra; DICTO's Bull-horns OG adaptor fills the whole gap with one piece, three per copy.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23451210
#35bThe DICTO Palintriacontahedron (2026)A short introduction: the icosidodecahedron's central pyramids flipped out at full height; the hull of the 32 tips, 60 triangles, each half a rhombic-dodecahedron rhombus: the member of the dodecahedron–icosahedron hull family at edge ratio 3/√5, exact. It interlocks with the next icosahedron, bridging one turn of the golden cycle to the next.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23430866
#35aThe DICTO Metaxyhexecontahedron (2026)A short introduction: the hull of an icosahedron and a dodecahedron at edge ratio 9/φ⁴, in closed form, exact; the solid that closes the golden cycle's last gap, touching the rhombic triacontahedron and the next icosahedron.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23430864
#35Vajrayana (2026)A companion to Progenitors: the dodecahedron returns inside the golden cycle as the hull of DICTO's Cleo star, filled exactly by 82 pieces of four kinds; the last loose step is closed by a new solid that touches both sides, the DICTO Metaxyhexecontahedron, each of its two layers one kind of piece; the cycle grows to twelve stations.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23429911
#34aThe DICTO Hecatogdoecontahedron (2026)A short introduction: the DICTO Hecatodihedron and its polar, the DICTO Ogdoecontahedron, interlocked in the truncated icosahedron's midsphere as one self-dual compound, 182 faces and 182 corners; core and hull are the classical rectified and joined truncated icosahedron; the pairing and the name are what is new.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23430868
#34Progenitors (2026)The DICTO Hecatodihedron and Ogdoecontahedron as a twin pair: on their own they never close golden; set into the Spheres of Dharma in the dodecahedron's place they close it again on φ⁹, and the cycle holds the core and hull of two dual compounds.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23413184
#33Double Vision – Both Cycles in One Space (2026)The Spheres of Dharma and their inside-out reversal in one space: one sphere of reciprocation toggles which shells the two cycles share, and no sphere gives both. Cut against each other they make one solid of exact convex pieces, 536 of 12 kinds per turn in one setting and 502 of 11 in the other.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23407107
#32Inverted Shells: The Spheres of Dharma Turned Inside Out (2026)Polar reciprocation turns the Spheres of Dharma inside out into a new exact golden cycle, every layer filled by one kind of piece. The great stellated dodecahedron locks into its outer gap at exactly one size, a dual pair fits its inner gap in reverse order, and the cycle repeats inward without end.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23405029
#31aThe DICTO OCD Stella (2026)A short introduction: the icosahedral hull of the golden adapter pieces round one Ogdoecontahedron in its body-centred cubic packing, cut along the 8 joints: 48 corners, 50 faces; copies fill exactly 151/2 − 267√5/8 ≈ 87.12% of space, and twelve round a small one fill an outer one exactly at φ².DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23430870
#31The DICTO Ogdoecontahedron (2026)The Spheres of Dharma turned inside out: the polar dual of the Hecatodihedron, a convex solid of 20 regular hexagons and 60 kites, exact in every coordinate. #30A Fibonacci Spine (2026)Five periodic packings of the DICTO Ogdoecontahedron meet round a five-fold axis; with the central copy, the copies removed along the axis are exactly the canonical Fibonacci chain, checked exactly, until minimal removal thins it at height 12.8.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23396361
#29Theaetetus (2026)An icosahedron held exactly by its Cleo-Icos, grown to the DICTO Hecatodihedron in two exact stories: 215 pieces in closed layers, and 102 clean pieces that cut the icosahedron and dodecahedron with the same twenty cones.DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23378070
