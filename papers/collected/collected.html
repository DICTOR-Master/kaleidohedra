All works, findings and names are DICTO's. Computation and checking with Claude (Anthropic).
About. DICTO is a geometric sculptor in Osaka whose work grows out of building with recycled PET and from three open apps on one shared geometry, Kaleidohedra, Rhombiverse and Polyhedraverse. This record gathers DICTO's published geometry under one DOI. Each work below has its own DOI, always pointing to its latest version.
