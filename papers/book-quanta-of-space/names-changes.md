# Quanta of Space (Volume I): names and credits, errors, clarity, repetition, 17 Nov 2026

Consolidation pass, batch 2 of the names audit, on the model of The DICTOnary of Atoms v1.4 (book-quanta-dictonary/names-changes.md). Draft Version 2.3 (Version 2.2: 7 November 2026).

Rule (DICTO, 17 Nov 2026): known shapes are given their regular names and credited to their discoverers. At first use in each entry, the regular name carries an asterisk and the discoverer and year, e.g. "Mosseri–Sadoc tile t6* (Mosseri and Sadoc, 1982)". The asterisk leads to a small note at the end of the entry (before its Data line), or at the end of the short entry in the short chapters. After the first use, only the regular short name is used: tile t6, tile t4, face pyramid, stellation 8, pseudoicosahedron, truncated octahedron x = φ / x = 1/φ, the regular octahedron. DICTO's genuinely new shapes keep his names. Note wordings are those settled in the DICTOnary v1.4.
