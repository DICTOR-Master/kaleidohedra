All the atoms

Every named shape of the work, one quantum of space each: first DICTO's new shapes, 102 of them, D1 to D102 (given in order of first publication, never reused); then his 31 new clusters, structures and networks built from them; the 23 known or rediscovered shapes the work builds with are listed at the end of the book. A shape whose model file is in the dataset DICTO Solids: Exact Geometry is drawn, all in one pose: gold for DICTO's new shapes, silver for known ones. Under each: its kind, the paper or finding it comes from (linked to its DICTO DOI), and the status found by the prior-art search.
