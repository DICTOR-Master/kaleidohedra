## Arc

- **Beginning:** the old question, what are the smallest exact pieces of space, and DICTO's answer as Postulates.
- **Middle:** the five orders walked in series order; each part states its principles and hands on to its books.
- **End:** the road through the cycle to *A Brief Geometry of Space*, which returns to these same pieces.
