Book plan: the Platonic solids
everything DICTO has found that belongs to the five, D · I · C · T · O: inside them, between them, filling space with them, and where they live in four dimensions

DICTO · plan of 11 November 2026 · for DICTO's decision · not for publication
PLAN, not a paper. Working title only (the title is DICTO's). Follows DICTO's note of 10 November ("covering all specific Platonic solids for the book"; the rhombic dodecahedron and the cuboctahedron went into the released paper, now #106) and absorbs the Platonic Lattices plan of 6 November, which DICTO approved in principle. Chapters are re-edited from the papers named, each cited; the papers stay where they are.

The arc. Plato gave the five solids to the elements; Euclid ended the Elements by building them and proving there are no more; Kepler nested them. DICTO's work takes the five apart and puts them back together: what lies inside each (cuts, cores, discs), how they fit together and fill space (every mix, one world), and where all five live at once (the 600-cell's corners).
