# The DICTO Tiles (draft abstract, 2026-10-12)

We collect five sets of convex prototiles, each of which tiles an icosahedral seed exactly. The DICTO Lotus tiles (icosahedron, AXE,
FUJI, CLEO) tile the icosahedron of edge φ² as 12 icosahedra of edge 1, one of edge 1/φ, 60 AXE, 20 FUJI and 20 CLEO; the dodecahedral
tiles (icosahedron, pentagonal pyramid J2, AXE, CLEO) tile the dodecahedron of edge 1; two Bodhi sets (rhombic triacontahedron with
Vajra, Crown Jewel and the chiral Diadems; or with Vajra, Chatra and Mudra) tile the triacontahedron of edge φ² around 13 smaller
triacontahedra; and ten tile types fill one cycle of the Spheres of Dharma, six nested icosahedral shells closing at φ³. In the Lotus,
Bodhi and Spheres of Dharma sets the seed is the set's own large solid enlarged, so each tiling continues without end, with the gap
tiles kept whole at every level: a hierarchy, not a substitution, since the gap tiles cannot be cut into smaller copies of the set
at one size (Dehn invariant). Every tile has exact coordinates in Q(√5); the tilings are checked pairwise free of overlap with exact
total volume. AXE and CLEO are the Mosseri–Sadoc tetrahedra t6 and t4, J2 is a Johnson solid, the Spheres of Dharma set uses
Mosseri–Sadoc's t1, and the twelve triacontahedra of the Bodhi seed sit around a rhombic hexecontahedron as in S. Kabai's clusters;
FUJI and the Bodhi tiles are new. The sets are described in DICTO, The Chain of Karma (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23310542), Spheres of
Dharma (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23310834) and The Bodhi Seed (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23311354).
