HexOct: Knitting Crystals

Paper #83 · Version 3 · 2 November 2026 · DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23848271 · Licence CC BY-SA 4.0

Withdrawn for revision. Versions 1 and 2 knitted an edge-on slice of the close-packed stackings: a picture of the crystal on a flat fabric. DICTO proposed something else from the start, which the title names: a fabric that is the crystal. One continuous multi-layer knitted fabric whose sheets are the close-packed layers, whose linking yarns are the bonds between neighbouring layers, with c and h carried by the linking itself, so that the HexOct c-tiles and h-tiles are the knitted cells through the fabric's thickness.

That knitted HexOct fabric is in preparation. It will be published as the next version of this record, under this same DICTO DOI. The earlier versions stay reachable through the version list for reference.
