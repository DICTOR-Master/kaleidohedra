# dictospheres.com card (draft, not on the site)

**HexOct: Knitting Crystals**

Image: `card-hexoct-knitting.png` (1200 × 900): the exact slice of the 4H stacking beside its knitting chart.

Text:
DICTO's idea: a knitted fabric that is an exact slice through a crystal of stacked balls. One plane cuts every
close-packed stacking into triangles and diamonds, coloured by the pieces between the balls: gold for cubic layers, blue
for hexagonal ones. Bias rows place each layer, so cubic stacking knits as a straight slant and hexagonal as a chevron.
Five swatch patterns (4H, 3C, 2H, 6H and the never-repeating Fibonacci stacking), with full row-by-row instructions.
Swatches not yet knitted: photos to come.
