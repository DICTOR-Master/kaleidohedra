## 1. #28 Hecatodihedron, next version

**Merit:** a needed correction. It is not a new result, but a published novelty statement needs this context.

**Honest headline:** the DICTO Hecatodihedron is the convex hull of the truncated icosahedron and the 20 three-fold vertices of its canonical dual. Put another way, it is the joined truncated icosahedron (jtI) with its 12 five-fold corners cut off at the pentagon planes (exact).

- **Does it affect the novelty statement? Yes.** #28 says "we did not find this flattened form" after checking the TI–PD compound. That is still literally true. But a reader must now be told that H comes directly from the jtI construction, applied to the hexagons only, and that the Shield height is the joined height. State it as "an unnamed solid derived from a known one". Do not call it a new kind of solid.
- **Fix "60 pairs" to "30 pairs"** of coplanar triangles (132 − 102 = 30 merges, 210 − 30 = 180 edges).
- **Fold in from Progenitors:** the H–O compound in the TI's midsphere (the DICTO Hecatogdoecontahedron: 182 faces, 182 corners, self-dual). Its core and hull are the same as those of the classical TI–PD compound: the rectified TI and the jtI. This belongs here, because it explains where H sits. It is too thin to carry a paper of its own. DICTO's name stays.
- **#31 note (for its next version):** the same fact, dualised, describes O. By polarity, O should be the canonical pentakis dodecahedron with its 20 three-fold corners cut by the TI's hexagon planes. This fits #31's kite angles (68.62° and 55.69° are the Catalan pentakis angles). Confirm it with one exact check before stating it. #31 already says "a named metric realisation, not a new combinatorial type", so this sharpens #31's claim and does not overturn it.
- **Before release:** check #29 and #31 for any wording like "new solid" about H. Cite Wikipedia's "Rhombic enneacontahedron" and McCooey's joined and rectified truncated icosahedron pages. Publish the new Collected Works version afterwards (the standing rule).
- **Risk:** low, as long as the new text says what H is and does not defend the old wording.

## 2. Golden Bodhi Pyrochlore

**Merit: solid.** With the golden-fill result added, this goes from a "design" paper to one with a clean exact theorem.

**Honest headline:** the gaps of the bcc Ogdoecontahedron packing have an exact 72-piece fill. Golden structure appears where the copies' limbs cross. The golden region provably cannot be filled by golden rhombohedra and Bodhi pieces alone. One adapter kind (the rhombic triacontahedron's three-fold corner cap, 24 per cell) removes every local obstruction, and whether the rest tiles is open.

- **Does the golden-fill result belong in this paper? Yes, before release.** It answers the paper's own open question. Without it, the conclusion ("a design rather than a theorem") undersells the work.
- **Add a new section after §5** with these parts:
  - The obstruction. It is exact: 24 star tips per cell in the four three-fold joint planes, and the area argument. The edge angle has cos² = φ²/3 (exact); write 20.905° as a float value.
  - The adapter. Exact, with zero overlaps.
  - G′. It has only golden walls, and its convex edges all lie along five-fold axes and open at 36°. Label the edge sampling and the angles as float.
  - The larger 22.6% region. No set of golden, Bodhi and Cleo-Ico pieces can fill it, because of the kite planes (exact).
- **Fix to match:**
  - §4 now gives the 20.9052° hexagon junction and the "low three-sided cap" as float. Upgrade both to exact and point to the new section.
  - §4 calls the Cleo-Ico "a ready-made adapter". Add that it is *not* the joint-tip adapter: its squared side-to-base ratio is the conjugate of the tip adapter's. Its place is the free hexagons.
  - Rewrite the abstract, the subtitle ("why a golden fill shatters" now tells only half the story) and §7's "Open" list. The open problem becomes "does G′ tile by golden rhombohedra?".
  - Status lines in §8: add the obstruction and the adapter as exact.
- **Drop or soften the periodic-fill bookkeeping.** "No periodic fill at brick edge (φ/9)|v|" is correct, but the scale is free, so it proves little. One sentence at most, worded as a check.
- **The new adapter piece has no name.** It is DICTO's to name, or he can leave it as "the tip adapter". Do not invent a name.
- **Risks:**
  - Overclaim: "no local obstruction" must not read as "it tiles".
  - Prior art: the proof is elementary, so present it as a short exact argument, not as a deep theorem.
  - Keep "not zonotopes" (already fixed).
