References

The classical and outside sources named in the hand-written parts of this book (the Opening, the chapter openings and bridges, Chapters 11 and 13, the Coda). The sources of each paper drawn on are in the Appendix under that paper.

  10. P. Kramer and R. Neri, On periodic and non-periodic space fillings of Em obtained by projection, Acta Cryst. A40 (1984) 580–587.

  16. J. H. Conway and S. Torquato, Packing, tiling, and covering with tetrahedra, PNAS 103 (2006) 10612–10617; J. H. Conway, Y. Jiao and S. Torquato, New family of tilings of three-dimensional Euclidean space by tetrahedra and octahedra, PNAS 108 (2011) 11009–11012.
  17. U. Betke and M. Henk, Densest lattice packings of 3-polytopes, Comput. Geom. 16 (2000) 157–186; S. Torquato and Y. Jiao, Dense packings of the Platonic and Archimedean solids, Nature 460 (2009) 876–879.

  20. R. W. Gray, the thirteen-icosahedron arrangement (2002), as cited in #19 and #22.
