Editorial rules

(1) One notation throughout: c and h layers, hexagonality h, cubicity 1 − h, the layer couplings J1, J2, J3 as in #68. (2) Each chapter is its paper, repeated introductions cut, theorems and proofs kept word for word, figures kept. (3) Later corrections carried in (for example, #73 says it completes the materials series; #74 followed). (4) One bibliography, each source marked read or cited as given; an index of materials and names. (5) A short opening for the general reader, as in Quanta of Space. (6) Published as its own Zenodo record (book) with a DICTO DOI, listed in Collected Works; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

Steps

1. Assemble the chapters from the paper sources. 2. Notation and correction pass. 3. Merge the bibliography and build the index. 4. Typeset; DICTO reads. 5. Publish.
