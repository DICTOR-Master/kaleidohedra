What this work holds

  1. Space is built from pieces.
  2. Every piece must be exact: a shape you could make and hold, checked to the last corner.
  3. A small kit is enough: a handful of shapes builds shells and spaces without end.
  4. The golden ratio decides when shells close.
  5. Pieces join in bonds, and the web of bonds is as real as the pieces.
  6. Repeating and never-repeating order meet in the pieces.
  7. Everything is open: the apps, the data and the papers, free to use and to build on.
