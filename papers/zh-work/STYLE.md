## Rules
1. Meaning: every claim, number, count, name, date, DOI, condition and qualifier of the English, correctly; nothing added. Numbers, formulas, coordinates and table values identical.
2. Simplified Chinese characters throughout (the zh.wikipedia extracts in wikipedia-zh/ are in traditional characters: convert).
3. Terms: glossary-zh.tsv (English → Chinese) is binding; build/extend it only via the lead's glossary pass. Standard geometry terms follow Chinese Wikipedia/textbook usage: regular polytope 正多胞体; 600-cell 正六百胞体; 120-cell 正一百二十胞体; tesseract 超正方体（正八胞体）; cell/face/edge/vertex 胞/面/棱/顶点; dual 对偶; uniform polytope 均匀多胞体; Platonic solids 柏拉图立体; Archimedean solids 阿基米德立体; truncated 截角; rectified 截半; bitruncated 过截角; icosidodecahedron 截半二十面体; cuboctahedron 立方八面体; snub dodecahedron 扭棱十二面体; rhombic dodecahedron 菱形十二面体; rhombic triacontahedron 菱形三十面体; Kepler–Poinsot polyhedra 开普勒-庞索多面体; golden ratio 黄金比 φ; golden cut (DICTO's: a cut of a solid) 黄金截切 (never 黄金分割, the golden section); space-filling 空间填充; honeycomb 蜂巢（空间填充）; slice 截面; shadow 影; close packing 密堆积; quasicrystal 准晶体; zonohedron 带状多面体.
4. DICTO's names: DICTO, AXE, CLEO, FUJI, EKP, HexOct, Kaleidohedra, Polyhedraverse stay in Latin. Other piece/shape names: translate the meaning where they are ordinary words (e.g. White Queen 白王后, Red Queen 红王后 — the standard names in Chinese translations of Through the Looking-Glass《爱丽丝镜中奇遇》; Golden Skin 黄金之皮; Void Keeper 空隙守护者), transliterate coined names (Kagemusha: 影武者, a Japanese word with standard Chinese characters). Give the English name once in （） at the first use in each book. The same shape has the same Chinese name everywhere (atom-names-zh.tsv).
5. No English words left in running text except Latin-capital names, DOIs/URLs, file paths, software names, reference lists (author names, journal and book titles stay in Latin), and a term with no settled Chinese form, which is described in Chinese with the English once in （）.
