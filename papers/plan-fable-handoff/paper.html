5. Rules that apply throughout

  • Naming policy (agreed 14 Nov with DICTO's corrections): description at first use; theorems readable without other papers; new names only when they recur or anchor a result; laws and methods carry a subtitle; DICTO at first mention per chapter, then only where it removes ambiguity; "DICTO DOI" unchanged; old names stay findable as "also called". Published records are not re-issued for wording alone.
  • Credit at the point of use: another author's method, code or result is credited in the sentence, caption or table where it is used (Stanley 1979, Critchlow 1969, Debrunner, Narayana), not only in the references.
  • Books first: release order Space-Fillers, Paradigm, Platonic, Brief Geometry. Each book is checked for its arc before release. New results are sorted to their own papers.
  • Read ok = publish: DICTO's "read ok" on a PDF releases that exact PDF the same session; a new record or anything beyond that PDF is asked first.
  • Deploy queue: site cards go through the 12:00 / 21:00 queue; master is never pushed by hand.
  • Exact before claimed: every lead is a claim until an exact test confirms it; the chat's "derived" items enter only as "Waiting for their volume".
  • Process record: a reproduction says "Reproduced" only when every numbered claim is checked, and a paper is proposed for reproduction only when it carries an author process record. Reproductions are closed for now.
  • Nothing sent outside without DICTO's word: OEIS, Palomar, Scholar, ORCID, funding and letters are DICTO's account; we prepare, he sends.
