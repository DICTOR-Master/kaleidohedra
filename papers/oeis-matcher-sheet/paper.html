DICTO · dictospheres.com · 8 November 2026 · Matcher run on dicto-big-brother over every study's data files; searches in the OEIS one every 3 seconds. Prepared by Claude (Anthropic) for DICTO.
Working sheet for DICTO, not for publication. New OEIS entries follow one at a time after A400949 is approved. Study: studies/2026-11-08-oeis-matcher (extract.py, match.py, candidates.json, matches.json) and studies/2026-11-08-sequences.

1. The run

1984 data files scanned; every list of six or more whole numbers stored as a count (shells, terms, loops, census, layers and the like) was kept, and lists of corner or face labels, of one repeated value, or of steady steps were left out: 96 lists. All 96 searched, no errors: 24 found, 72 not found.

  • Already prepared or assessed on 7 November: the 4H and 6H coordination sequences and the fcc loop counts (OEIS entries 2–7 waiting behind A400949), and the SU(4), SO(7), SO(9) Fibonacci multiplet dimensions and the 4F(3n+1), 4F(3n+2) counts (assessed, not chosen).

  • Noise: paper and reference numbers caught in a sentence, a continued fraction, a list of primes, and face-to-face gluing counts that belong to one chosen library of pieces, not to a natural question.
