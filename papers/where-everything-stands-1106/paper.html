Findings of the night (in the studies, not yet in papers)

EKP is one of the fourteen chains (dodecahedron ⊃ cube ⊃ stella octangula ⊃ octahedron ⊃ icosahedron) in a simple cubic lattice; it is NOT the 1/1 approximant (that is body-centred), a claim I had made too early and withdrew. Crossed nest: stella → octahedron; five cubes → rhombic triacontahedron; ten tetrahedra → icosahedron; five octahedra in the icosahedron → an icosahedron 1/φ smaller, the crossed golden loop. Overlap: icosahedra in the octet touch at √5/2 and fill all space at exactly φ; the octet's roofs form at 1 + √5/2 and φ²; dodecahedra touch at 3/2 and their roofs meet at 3. Exactness audit of #99: every factor and size exact in Q(√2, √5); tile counts exact on every rung (13/8: 27 060 + 16 724).
