Star Cores: book plan

Plan for DICTO's fifth book: spiked cores, their halos and the cycles they start

DICTO · plan of 30 October 2026 · for DICTO's decision · not for publication

1. What the book shows

DICTO's TI Star (#78) turned a solid's own pyramids outwards and found that the spikes start a cycle. Every such step, spike the faces and wrap the tips, is DICTO's growth rule T of Universal PiTet (#38) run backwards. So every solid has a star chain, and the book asks which chains close, how fast they grow and what cores they pass through.

Scope (DICTO, 30 October): every new shape gets the star core treatment. Each of DICTO's new shapes with a model in the data set gets the treatment #78 gave TI: (1) its own pyramids from the centre; (2) the spikes, mirrored (×2) and golden (×φ); (3) the star, its hull of tips (the halo) and the exact fills, with overlaps checked; (4) the core of the turn, one solid per family of face planes, intersected; (5) the core's own turn (does it return?); (6) the star chain. The result is one card per shape in the book's catalogue.
Main statement (the book's aim, to be proved in Part III). A solid whose faces lie in just two families of planes, on the five-fold and three-fold axes (or the four-fold and three-fold), has a star cycle in which the cores alternate with period two and the hulls with period four, as the truncated icosahedron does (#78, Propositions 4 and 5). Solids with more kinds of face plane run in longer chains, some of which pass through DICTO's named solids.

4. What is already known (survey v2, 55 solids of the data set)

  • Period four, like TI: the GTI, the FPD and the Palintriacontahedron (one orbit, #38), Icosaxes, Unborn Axes, the Metaxyhexecontahedron, Tweedledum and Samson.
  • Period two: David and Delilah.
  • Long chains through named solids: TQHD → TQP → TQH → TPH → OG (#39), and OG → KRE → PHE → TPP → DPHP, after which the chain leaves the data set.
  • Not closed in eight steps: 45 solids, among them the Queens' slices (Alice and the others). These are screened only: the step is valid only where the star lies inside its hull.
  • Found on the way: the FPD (#38) and the Palintriacontahedron have the same shape.

6. Decisions

  • FPD and Palintriacontahedron, one shape. Pick: keep FPD (#38, the first name), with "Palintriacontahedron" noted as its other name. No D-number changes.
  • Overlap with The Golden Cycle. #54 and #78 are in both books. Pick: keep both, since a book should read whole.
  • Order of work. Pick: the studies first, then papers A, B and C, then the book. Each paper is released when ready, and the book comes after paper C.
  • An app feature for every paper. Pick: a "Star step" button in Polyhedraverse that spikes any convex solid and wraps it, one tap per step. It is the View-in-app target for A, B and C.
