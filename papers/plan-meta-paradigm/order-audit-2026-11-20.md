# Order audit of the books, 20 November 2026

Read-only audit for DICTO's question: "check whether similar adjustments are needed in atoms or other books". Nothing in any book was changed. The standard used is the one being planned for Quanta of Space I: each book shows its place in The Five Orders of Space; it presents its own order's subject and not the whole work; it speaks of the orders in series order, cube green (Books One–Two), icosahedron blue (Three–Five), tetrahedron red (Six–Seven), octahedron yellow (Eight), dodecahedron violet (Nine–Eleven); and it hands each other subject on to the book where that subject is developed.

Method: the current `paper.html` of every book folder, with the shared series page left out. Headings were listed; mentions of other books were found by title and by "Book N"; chapters with the same subject in two books were compared by shared runs of eight words (the % is the share of each chapter's eight-word runs found in the other); element words were searched (fire, water, earth, wind, air, void, aether). The home-book count for the DICTOnary is a rough count from source-paper numbers, not a finished mapping.

## Recommendation, in order of priority

1. The DICTOnary follows Quanta I in the same version pass: its "Told in Volume I, Chapter N" field (181 cards and the structures table) and its work-by-work group headings point to Quanta's six topic chapters and will be wrong once Quanta is reordered. Rewrite that one field as "Developed in: Book N, order", rename and reorder the group headings by the five orders, add a one-table index by order, the series line and the "Definitions" line. No regrouping of the catalogue itself.
2. One question for DICTO before that: the home book of the atoms with no book yet, above all the Aequi and the Seraphim (#112) and the #96 pieces; until he places them, "not yet in a book".
3. Bonds, The Golden Ladder and the Queens hold the most chapters of other books without a pointer (four near-verbatim pairs: Bonds 3 = Ladder 3B, Bonds 11 = HexOct 1, Golden Cycle 13 = Star Cores 1, Ladder 5 = Queens 8). At each one's next real version: series line and page plus a "developed in Book N" line at the head of each such chapter.
4. The Platonic Solids: element words as colour names and as labels in the Coda's last paragraphs, against the 16 November rule; fix at its next version.
5. The five pre-series books (Three to Six, Nine) still list the other books in order of publication in the author note and use "Cycles of Growth, Part I/II"; put the lists in series order and name Book Four for "Part I" when each is next versioned. The Paradigm's section 0.7 and its "home Book N, Chapter M" pointers are the model to copy.
