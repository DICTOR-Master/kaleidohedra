## Points

**1. MAJOR — Observation 1's "open problem" is a one-paragraph theorem.** Location: Observation 1 (§5), In short ("In every two-kind network computed, the bond between unlike pieces never breaks"), §6 and §7 ("We found no general statement"). Problem: for two kinds the unlike bond *must* exist at every parameter where both kinds have cells of positive volume, in any face-to-face tiling by convex cells, periodic or not. Proof: let U_A be the union of the A cells (closed, locally finite union). An interior point of a B cell lies in no A cell (cells have disjoint interiors), so U_A is a proper closed subset of ℝ^d with nonempty interior, hence ∂U_A ≠ ∅, and ℝ^d ∖ ∂U_A = int U_A ⊔ int U_B is disconnected. A closed polyhedral set of dimension ≤ d − 2 cannot disconnect ℝ^d, so ∂U_A contains a relatively open piece of a facet of some A cell, of positive (d − 1)-measure. Points on the far side of that piece lie in B cells (locally finitely many), so one B cell covers a positive-measure part of that facet; as the diagram is face-to-face, that part is a common facet: an A–B bond. ∎ With three kinds the same argument gives only that each kind is bonded to *some* other kind, which is exactly what the perovskite shows (A–B dies, A–X and B–X survive). Evidence: the argument above (no computation needed); consistent with all ten walks. Fix: replace Observation 1 by "Proposition (two kinds): in every face-to-face tiling by convex cells of two kinds the unlike bond exists wherever both kinds have cells of positive volume" with the proof, state the three-kind corollary ("every kind keeps an unlike bond"), change the In short sentence to "...never breaks, and we prove that it never can", and drop "We found no general statement" (or keep it as "we found no statement of this elementary fact in the sources searched"). This is also what DICTO's theorem-driven rule wants.

Problem: (i) and §2 define coexistence by cells of positive volume and use the *open* interval; on the rectangle's edges one kind has shrunk to points (A at λ = −2, X at λ = 2 and at μ = 1, B at μ = −1; my probes and `perovskite.json` "corners": (0, 1) has X vanished, (2, −1) B and X, (0, −1) B). **3. MINOR — Observation 3's description of the zinc blende sites is literally false.** Location: Observation 3, Remark: "Zn: the points of 2ℤ³ with even coordinate sum". **4. MINOR — Observation 5's hcp shift has a two-line exact explanation; "was not examined" can go.** Location: Observation 5; README "Notes per network 5". Evidence: my walk shows the tet cell near λ = −11/24 shrinking to the point (0, 0, c/2) (centroid of the last cell = (0, 0, 0.8164), c/2 = 0.8165), the centre of the triangle shared by the two face-sharing tetrahedral holes.
