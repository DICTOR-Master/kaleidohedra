Built from the 266 study folders: 143 are about the Platonic solids; 114 of these already feed published papers, 29 have never been written up. Chapter contents below are the candidate results, each with its study; the order and the title are DICTO's to set.

The idea

The papers each prove one theorem. The studies behind them found much more: exact results that never needed a paper of their own, results that are complete only together, and careful proofs that something cannot be done. DICTO STUDIES collects these for the five Platonic solids, re-curated, with the method shown: exact arithmetic, every search with a control it must pass and one it must fail.

Part I. One world

  1. Every mix at free scale. All 31 mixes of the five solids fill space in two worlds joined by one face-to-face seam, with six connectors (#47 needed four worlds and seven). study 2026-11-10-free-scale-mixes
  2. Cubes as cubes. Next to every Dogstar the edging cell is a whole cube; the Dogstar keeps four or five roofs; the kit drops to five pieces. same study, step 2
  3. One world for all. Each solid inside a skin that makes it a dodecahedral cell (Euclid: cube + six roofs; tetrahedron + four corners; octahedron + eight caps; icosahedron in its Karma shell), so every mix lives on one cubic grid: no seam at all. Exact volumes. 2026-11-10-one-world
  4. Sphere-unit sizes. Dodecahedron, cube and tetrahedron share one sphere (radius 1.401); octahedron on the roof ridges (1.309); icosahedron of edge 1 in Pacioli's place with the Admiral Caps (each exactly φ/12). same study
  5. The flat Chain of Karma. A plane through the dodecahedron world at level φ/(2√3) cuts every crossed Dogstar into an exact CLEO; the octet's Pacioli icosahedra sit face to face on the CLEO bases (all 60 checked). 2026-11-10-di-bridge
