The arc. Plato's solids do not fill space, but nested and crossed they fill lattices. Each chapter builds one of the classical chains of inscriptions into a periodic structure, and the last ones climb from cubic order to the five-fold order of quasicrystals, whose source is E8, the top rung of The Golden Ladder.

The spine: one catalogue, several lattices
