The arc, in one line each

Beginning. DICTO's radial cell projection and the pieces that grew from it (Part I). Middle. Shadows of the fourth dimension, then the two Queens themselves (Parts II and III). End, new. The shapes found along the way turn out to be hidden in the Queens' own walls: the silent sets are the White Queen's corners, their plates are her walls, and in the Red Queen many overlapping copies share one wall system.
