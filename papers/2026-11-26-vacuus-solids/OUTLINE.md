# Vacuus-Solids: building out the centre of the Vacuus (outline, 26 Nov 2026)

DICTO · paper · with Claude (Anthropic). Scope approved by DICTO 26 Nov (15 items, thread C). Gold standard #43 v3; Winston style:
promise, vision early, cycling, fence + near misses, signposts, close on contributions; Pledge-Turn-Prestige.

## Arc (one line per section)
- **Pledge.** The Vacuus of a solid is a set of plates meeting at one point; DICTO: "a true vacuus or resonus barely exists". Grow that
  point into a core (the solid's dual, scaled) and every plate becomes a pit wall: a whole family of buildable solids, the Vacuus-Solids.
- **Turn.** Along each family only a few sizes are special, where every wall lies on a core solid's faces: exactly twelve, all quadratic
  in √2 or √5, and with the dual as core only the two classics, Wenninger's excavated dodecahedron and Poinsot's great dodecahedron.
- **Prestige.** The special sizes are where building out meets stellation (Kepler, Poinsot, Cauchy), their depths are golden or silver
  (√5/φ³, 3/φ³, 2/φ², 3/δ²), and one solid, the rhombic triacontahedron, builds a 1/φ copy of itself.
