#88Every Platonic Solid Has Exactly Two Golden Cuts

DICTO observed that one face runs through his recent solids: the truncated triangle, a hexagon with all six angles 120° and its sides alternating between two lengths. Among the solids of the data set DICTO Solids, every hexagon with three-fold symmetry is one, and in three single solids, Icosaxes, Unborn Axes and White Knight, its two sides are exactly in the golden ratio φ: these are the DICTO-GTT-Solids. Cut every corner of a Platonic solid by the same amount so that each face becomes a golden truncated polygon, and there are exactly two ways for each solid, ten solids in all: the DICTO-Kepler golden truncations, the Greater and Lesser Golden Cut solids. At the octahedron they are DICTO's Icosaxes and Unborn Axes, the pair that fills space together (#48); the other eight are new within the checks of #88, and stand in the catalogue of Volume II. The icosahedral and octahedral pairs are slices of the 120-cell and the 24-cell at 1/φ² and 1/φ of the way from the centre to a cell, either side of the Archimedean slice at one half. Names by DICTO.
