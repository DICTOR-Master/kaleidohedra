Introduction

How to read an entry

This is a dictionary of the atoms of space: every named shape of DICTO's geometric work, one quantum of space each. Volume I, Quanta of Space: A Manifesto, tells the works in six chapters; this volume holds their shapes and their numbers. The name is DICTO's: the DICTOnary.

Each entry is read like an entry in a dictionary.

Headword: the D-number and the name
D1, D2 … are DICTO's permanent numbers for his new single solids and pieces, given in order of first publication and never reused. A known shape keeps the number its first maker gave it (Johnson, uniform, Wenninger, Hart, Mosseri–Sadoc). Clusters, structures and networks built from the pieces carry no number (—).
Definition: the picture, the data and the source
Picture. Drawn from the exact model file in the data set DICTO Solids: Exact Geometry, all in one pose: gold for DICTO's new shapes, silver for known ones. A shape with no model file yet is listed in a table under “Not drawn here”.
Data. Its kind (solid, piece, cluster, structure, network, compound or curved body) and the status found by the prior-art search. “Not found” means only that the sources searched did not show it; it is not proof of novelty. The exact values of each work, its volumes, proof sketches and checks, are in “The data, work by work”, one section per work: A.18 is the data of paper #18.
Source. The paper by its number (#41) or the Kaleidohedra finding (F1), linked to its DICTO DOI, and the chapter of Volume I that tells it (Volume I, Chapter 1).

The parts of this volume

All the atoms: DICTO's 134 new shapes, then his 37 new clusters, structures and networks, then those not drawn yet. The data, work by work: the sections A.8 to A.51, with A.DB for the DICTONIC SOLIDS and BONDS, A.K for the Kaleidohedra findings and A.D for the data set. Known and rediscovered shapes: the 27 shapes the work builds with that were made first by others. Every value can be rebuilt and checked from the data set, whose checker re-proves it from the data files alone.
