Five small choices

  1. Name. "Mandala World" is a placeholder; the name is yours.
  2. Turn. The slider is a half turn with three still stops. A full turn would give six (the yantra repeats every 60°). Recommended: keep the half turn.
  3. At the stops. The narrowed yantra is drawn flat in front of the tetrahedra (exact in any view). Alternative: switch the whole view to parallel projection at the stops. Recommended: keep it as it is.
  4. After a stop. Letting go between stops glides the camera back to the 3D view. Alternative: leave the camera where it is. Recommended: glide back.
  5. Inside the spheres. The Cleo sphere is empty inside. Should the Cleo-Icos (or the icosahedron) be shown inside it, as a see-through shell with the solid within? Recommended: yes, the Cleo-Icos.
