The DICTO OCD Stella

The Ogdoecontahedron's companion cut along its joints: a golden solid that fills 151/2 − 267√5/8 ≈ 87.12% of space in the Ogdoecontahedron's packing, copies meeting face to face

Abstract. The DICTO Ogdoecontahedron (OCD) packs in a body-centred cubic arrangement, each copy joined to 8 neighbours on hexagons. The golden adapter pieces around one copy have an icosahedral hull, the OCD's companion C (72 corners: 30 hexagons in golden planes and 20 triangles). Cutting C along the 8 joints gives the DICTO OCD Stella S: 48 corners and 50 faces, golden except for the joint triangles, which are exactly the packing cell's hexagon walls. Copies of S meet face to face across all 8 joints and fill exactly 151/2 − 267√5/8 ≈ 87.12% of space. Twelve OCD Stellas around one small one fill an outer OCD Stella exactly at φ², the Bodhi seed's pattern, though S is only pyritohedral. Exact in ℚ(√5) unless marked float.

1. The solid

Around one OCD of its packing (cell volume 512/729 + 256√5/729), the 60 golden adapter pieces (the full orbit of the 24 per cell) have a convex hull C with 72 corners, 30 hexagons in the golden planes of the OCD's envelope and 20 triangles in the three-fold planes through the adapters' outer corners (squared distance 14/27 − 10√5/243), volume (−18880 + 9760√5)/2187. Its combinatorial type is Conway t3jD, the triacontahedron truncated at its 20 three-fold corners, here at the depth set by the OCD (Antiprism). The DICTO OCD Stella is S = C cut by the packing's Voronoi cell, that is, along the 8 joints.

2. The seed and the crystals

The seed (exact). An outer OCD Stella of scale φ² holds 12 OCD Stellas on its five-fold axes, each touching it, and one of scale 1/φ at the centre touching all 12. By the Minkowski argument the seed closes exactly when no two of the twelve overlap; of the 66 pairs, 30 have gauge exactly 1 (touching, each copy 5 others, along the icosahedron's edges) and 36 exactly φ. So it closes exactly at φ² with the centre at 1/φ, and the gap is −69 + 31√5 ≈ 31.8%: the pattern of the Bodhi Seed (DICTO DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23311353), though the OCD Stella is only pyritohedral (stella_seed_exact.py).

Crystals. On one network of the OCD's packing with a partner on the other: with icosahedra in the OCD's 20 hexagon planes, every solid meets its 8 joint neighbours face to face and the fill is 1523/32 − 669√5/32 ≈ 84.60% (exact); with regular octahedra, ≈ 96.33% (float); alone on both networks, 87.12% (exact). With icosahedra or octahedra the crystal has space group Fd-3 (No. 203).

3. Status

Known: the combinatorial type of C (Conway t3jD, the chamfered-icosahedron family). Not yet searched comprehensively: the OCD Stella itself, its packing and its seed. We state no priority claim here beyond "derived by DICTO from the Ogdoecontahedron's packing"; a prior-art search is to follow.
