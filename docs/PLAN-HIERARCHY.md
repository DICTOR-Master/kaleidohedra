# Plan: one hierarchy of dual pairs (Lotus × Dharma × Bodhi) — start 2026-10-13

**Idea (DICTO, 2026-10-12):** join the three published structures without changing any of them. The Chain of Karma (#22) works with
icosahedron ↔ dodecahedron; Spheres of Dharma (#23) runs from the icosidodecahedron (core) to its dual, the rhombic triacontahedron
(outer); the Bodhi seed (#24) multiplies the triacontahedron. If every triacontahedron in a Bodhi seed opens into a Dharma cycle,
and its icosahedra into Lotus seeds, the three become one structure in which each dual pair hands over to the next.

**Rules:** one job at a time, node for anything heavy, stop each job when its result is in; frugal with tokens; no Fable unless
DICTO says so for a specific step. The published papers are not changed; a positive result gets its own paper citing them.

## Steps
1. **Sizes (minutes, local).** In the Dharma cycle the outer triacontahedron has edge ½√(45 − 18√5) = 1.0898 when the cycle's
   icosahedra are 1/φ and φ². Scale it to the Bodhi seed's RT(1) and RT(1/φ): which icosahedra, dodecahedra and cages appear,
   at which sizes, and are they golden powers that the Lotus seed / Chain can continue? (Expected: a ratio of 1.0898 means the
   Dharma icosahedra inside an RT(1) are not at golden sizes; then step 2 asks for a nearby exact fit.)
2. **Fit test (local).** If the sizes are not golden, test the nearest exact options: (a) a Dharma cycle scaled so its RT is exactly
   RT(1), giving icosahedra of edge 0.5671 and 2.4022 — record whether any Lotus/Bodhi size matches; (b) the RT built round other
   shells (the cage, the dodecahedron) at golden sizes, from the RT/cage and D/cage relations already checked.
3. **Whole-structure check (node).** For the version that closes: build two levels (a Bodhi seed whose 13 RT are opened into Dharma
   cycles, whose icosahedra are opened into Lotus seeds), then the standard checks: no overlapping pair (separating axes), every piece
   inside, total volume exact.
4. **Counts and pictures.** Pieces per level by type; the dual pairs at each hand-over; one figure of the nested hierarchy (crystal
   shells, pulled apart) with the existing renderer.
5. **Write-up** (only if 3 passes): short paper "One hierarchy of dual pairs", DICTO's approval, then Zenodo on his word.
