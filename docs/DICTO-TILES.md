# The DICTO Tiles

DICTO's tile sets: the prototiles from which each of his structures is tiled exactly, like Mosseri and Sadoc's tetrahedra or
Danzer's ABCK, with the credit for every tile that is known from elsewhere.

Each set tiles its seed exactly. In the Lotus, Bodhi and Spheres of Dharma sets the seed is the set's own large solid enlarged
(icosahedron, triacontahedron, icosahedron), so the structure continues without end (the Chain of Karma, the Bodhi chain, the Ritsudō
cycle); the gap tiles stay whole at each step and accumulate in every size (they do not themselves inflate: Dehn invariant). The
dodecahedral set fills the dodecahedron, which is not one of its tiles.

## The Lotus tiles at every scale
The Lotus seed dissects the icosahedron into 13 smaller icosahedra (ratios 1/φ² and 1/φ³) and AXE, FUJI and CLEO. Applied to every
icosahedron at every scale it gives an exact hierarchical (iterated) dissection in which only the icosahedron is subdivided; AXE,
FUJI and CLEO stay whole and occur at every scale φ^k. It is not a substitution tiling in the strict sense, which would require
every prototile to be subdivided (no such requirement applies to hierarchical dissections). Downward, the icosahedra's share of the
volume tends to 0 and only the finite stages are tilings in the strict sense; upward, pieces of unbounded size occur.

## The tiles
Edge lengths and volumes are at edge 1 (the Lotus seed's icosahedra), φ = (1 + √5)/2. Names are DICTO's. Started 2026-10-12;
to be extended as pieces are named.

### Classical solids used throughout
Regular icosahedron and dodecahedron, icosidodecahedron, truncated icosahedron (fullerene cage), rhombic triacontahedron, rhombic
hexecontahedron, prolate and oblate golden rhombohedra.
