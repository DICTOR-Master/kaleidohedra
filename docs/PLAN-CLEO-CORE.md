## What is already known (facts, 2026-10-15)

- The hollow is the icosidodecahedron of edge 1 (ID). An icosahedron of edge 1 (I) on its five-fold axes sits inside with its 20 face
  centres exactly at the points of 20 Cleo-Icos standing on the ID's triangles (cleo_hold.py, locked against every turn and shift).
- The I is itself 20 Cleo-Icos meeting at the centre, so I + 20 holders = 40 identical Cleo-Icos; left empty: exactly 5 + 2√5.
- Each core corner stops 0.425325 short of its pentagonal window: room for a flat pentagonal pyramid (base the window, point on the
  corner). Over each core face, between it and the ID triangle (parallel, turned 180°, distance φ²/(2√3)), is a triangular antiprism.
- Our octahedral network variant (Spheres of Dharma work, 2026-10-12) filled the step I → ID exactly with 12 pyramids + 20 twisted
  frusta: the same decomposition, to be confirmed at these sizes.
