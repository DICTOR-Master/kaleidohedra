# Sources

*Literature behind DISCOVERIES.md and TARGETS.md. This file gives citations and where to read each source. The repo holds no copies of third-party papers.*

## Grünbaum (2010): parallelohedra, zonohedra and their types

Branko Grünbaum, "The Bilinski dodecahedron, and assorted parallelohedra, zonohedra, monohedra, isozonohedra and otherhedra" (manuscript, version 2/3/10, 30 pages).

- Read: https://digital.lib.washington.edu/researchworks/bitstreams/a3bd8e7b-a9ec-48be-890f-cb2d3ef96c08/download
- Covers: the five combinatorial types of parallelohedra (Figure 2; the regular-hexagon elongated dodecahedron, Fig. 2(b), and the truncated octahedron, Fig. 2(a)), and Fedorov's enumeration.
- Does not cover: face angles of the sheared hexagonal-prism family, so it cannot settle the hexagon corners in TARGETS.md (rows 1, 2, 6, 7, 9–12).
- Cited in DISCOVERIES.md, findings #5, #6 and #9.

## Fedorov (1885): *Elements of the Theory of Figures*

E. S. Fedorov, "Elements of the Theory of Figures" (Russian), *Zapiski Mineralogicheskogo Obshchestva* (vol. 21), 1885.

- Read: no verified free copy yet. Russian digital libraries (Math-Net.ru, archive.org) are the next places to look.
- Covers: the original classification of parallelohedra into five types, and the figure definitions Grünbaum uses.
- Cited in DISCOVERIES.md, finding #9.
