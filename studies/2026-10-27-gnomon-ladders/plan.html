Gnomon Ladders

Stage 0 plan: building the exact angles of the White Queen 600-cell from gnomons (placeholder name)

DICTO · idea 27 October 2026: "square roots could use gnomons" · study 2026-10-27-gnomon-ladders
Goal. Every exact angle found for the 600-cell's six turnings has a cosine √(a + b√5) with a, b rational. Each such number can be built with straightedge and compass. DICTO's idea: build each one from gnomons — the pieces that grow a figure into a larger similar figure (the L-shape that grows a square into the next square; the golden gnomon, the 36°–36°–108° triangle, that grows a golden triangle into the next one). Result wanted: for each angle, a short exact construction ("ladder") made only of gnomon steps, drawn and checked.

Stages

Stage 0 — now. This plan; the inventory above (exact, from shadow_turnings_exact.json); prior art; decisions below.

Stage 1 — the moves. Fix a small set of gnomon steps, each exact: (G1) square gnomon (n² → (n+1)²); (G2) golden gnomon (golden triangle → the next, ratio φ); (G3) the right-triangle step that takes a square root (Euclid VI.13, the geometric mean; the Theodorus spiral is a chain of such gnomon triangles); (G4) adding or halving a length. Prove each step exact in ℚ(√5) and its square roots.

Stage 2 — the ladders. For each angle, search for the shortest chain of moves that reaches it, starting from a unit and φ; check every chain exactly (each length and angle as a + b√5 or its root). Record the step count. Look for shared rungs: the turnings already share angles, so the ladders may share steps.

Stage 3 — pictures. One drawing per ladder (gnomons in gold on the dark ground, DICTO mark), and one plate with all eighteen. Then a "Gnomon" panel in the White Queen world beside the Shadow view: the current angle drawn as its ladder.

Stage 4 — write-up. Where it goes is DICTO's choice (a section of #63 or its own short paper); asked when Stage 3 is done.
