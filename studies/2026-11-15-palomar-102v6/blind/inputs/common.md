## Setting (shared by all statements)
Let T be the triangular lattice in the plane and A, B, C its three cosets of close-packed positions. A stacking is a bi-infinite word s = (s_k) over {A, B, C} with s_{k+1} != s_k, periodic or not; bilayer k is "h" if s_{k-1} = s_{k+1}, else "c". The tetrahedral network G_s has, at each bilayer k, a lower and an upper atom at every point of coset s_k, a vertical bond between them, and each upper atom bonded to the three nearest lower atoms of bilayer k+1 (every atom has degree 4). ABC... gives the diamond network (3C), AB... lonsdaleite (2H); other words give polytypes (4H, 6H, 9R, ...) and aperiodic stackings. A polygon through a site v is a simple cycle of G_s containing v; p_l(v; s) is the number of polygons of length l through v.

Source: DICTO, "Loop Equality" (#102), Version 6 draft (Zenodo preprint series; earlier versions not peer reviewed). Version 1 proved that closed non-backtracking loop counts are stacking-independent at every site; Version 5 reported (by exact enumeration) that polygon counts through a site agree for l <= 16 and first differ at l = 18. Version 6 adds the proofs below. Known background the paper cites: Whitney 2-isomorphism twists (1933); diamond polygon counts (Guttmann–Jensen; OEIS A038515); ring statistics of ice Ih/Ic (Herrero–Ramírez 2013); Benito–Urrutia–Karayiannis–Laso, Crystals 13 (2023) 1316 (SAW counts on hcp vs fcc sphere packings differ beyond some length, by enumeration).
