# App worlds audit, 21 Nov 2026 (read-only; Kaleidohedra app, master)

Sources: the world menu by the books (src/app/book-menu-data.js on master, which lists each world's papers), the wizard's lists (K_WORLDS,
LATTICES_3D, LATTICES_4D, 1D/2D families, 5D/6D catalogues in dimension-wizard.js), the Concordance (113 papers), the survey map, and a text
search of every paper and book source for each world's name and shape. The Zenodo link map used by zen_applink.py is an input file passed on the
command line and is not kept in the repository, so the menu's paper lists stand in for it. "Covered" = a paper or book describes the world by name or
shape or links to it. "(thin)" = covered, but only by a mention or a sibling world, so a figure from the app would add something.

COUNTS: worlds total 63 | covered 52 (of which 6 thin) | uncovered 11.
(63 = 37 worlds in the K list incl. shells, targets, studies, golden, sunstar, stella-jewel, breathing, bodhi-pyro, octet-build, roof-fold;
 13 lattices; 3 films/kits; 3 four-dimensional lattices; 5D and 6D; 5 in 1D/2D.)

## Top 5 uncovered worlds most worth a paper or a figure

1. Targets: the exact search and the 160 cells are already done (TARGETS.md, DISCOVERIES.md), only the paper is missing; it also closes the loop with #1, #5, #25 and #49.
2. Nets: connects directly to the Dehn Canon (#113) and #116 (a net folding to a solid), and a figure is cheap.
3. Kaleidoscope: a clean statement (which tile sets close under mirrors) that links to the Mirror Law and the Window Law.
4. 5D quasicrystal with the 6D quasicrystal: one paper "Penrose from five dimensions, golden rhombohedra from six", completing the Golden Rhombohedra world, which is covered only by name.
5. Z4 (Hypercubic): the missing base case of the scutoid tissues ladder (square, cube, tesseract), one figure for #104 and #114 at least.

Also worth one chapter figure: Construct and Signal for book Eleven (1D start of "A Brief Geometry of Space").
