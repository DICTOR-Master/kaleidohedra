# The GOLDEN square (book cover)

DICTO's request (2026-10-29): a cover for "The Golden Cycle" in the HexOct style.

**Conclusion:** first version made, golden-cover-front.png (square + title). Status: shown to DICTO.

- Cyclic Latin square: GOLDEN across the top and down the left, each letter once in every row and column.
- One letter per station of the Ritsudō cycle of Spheres of Dharma (#23), in order: G icosahedron (blue), O icosidodecahedron (teal), L dodecahedron (violet), D truncated icosahedron, the fullerene cage (silver), E rhombic triacontahedron (gold), N the icosahedron again, φ³ larger, closing the cycle (blue). All at one circumradius and one pose; face counts checked (20, 32, 12, 32, 30).
- Letters and title: the HexOct LED letters (DSEG14 Classic, OFL), gaps closed in the title, larger initials; ◆ DICTO ◆.
