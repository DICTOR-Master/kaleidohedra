# Cycles world, stage 1: stations already in the checked builds (DICTO, 2026-10-14)

`scripts/stations.py` → `stations-measured.json`: every closed convex stage of the three fused-shells builds, measured from
their pieces (sizes against the starting icosahedron of edge 1/φ; outline circumradius).

**The Dharma build is already a loop of named solids**, in this order outward:

| Dharma stage | Solid | Size (outline radius ÷ start) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | icosahedron, edge 1/φ | 1 |
| 2 | icosidodecahedron, edge φ/2 | 2.227 |
| 3 | dodecahedron, edge 1 | 2.384 |
| 5 | truncated icosahedron, edge 1/φ | 2.606 |
| 6 | DICTO Truncated-Icosahedral Hecatodihedron | 2.732 |
| 8 | rhombic triacontahedron | 3.000 |
| 10 | icosahedron, edge φ² | **φ³ = 4.236** |

It ends on the starting icosahedron, φ³ larger. The Lotus seed (Karma) ends on the **same** icosahedron (edge φ², radius 2.490),
and Bodhi Kit B grows its triacontahedron by the same **φ³**. So all three builds grow by φ³ per pass.

Correction to PLAN-CYCLES.md: "outline = icosahedron φ² larger" is the outline's **edge** (φ², with the core dodecahedron's edge 1);
against the starting icosahedron the growth factor is **φ³**.

For DICTO to decide: the Dharma order (icosidodecahedron before the dodecahedron; truncated icosahedron and Hecatodihedron before
the triacontahedron) differs from the plan's Loop 1 order, and Dharma grows as it goes, so it is a growth loop, not a one-size loop.

## Great icosahedron: buildable scope (2026-10-14)

`scripts/great_ico_cells.py` → `great-ico-cells.json`: the icosahedron's 20 face planes cut the great icosahedron into **413 cells of 10
kinds** (core icosahedron 1; then 20, 30, 60, 60, 20, 12, 120, 30, 60 by distance from the centre); their volume 110.70 matches a Monte
Carlo volume of the great icosahedron (110.77) with the core's inradius 1. Building options for DICTO: the stellation cells layer by layer
(faithful to the stellation diagram, 413 pieces), or a core icosahedron + 20 congruent face units (21 pieces).
