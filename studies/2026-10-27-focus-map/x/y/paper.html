The one piece. Everything below is built from the octet pair: the regular tetrahedron and the regular octahedron. A tetrahedron is an octahedron with four small tetrahedra on alternate faces; a regular octahedron sits inside every tetrahedron, at the midpoints of its edges. The rule of halves shares space into such pieces, whole or halved; the 600-cell is 600 tetrahedra, and each hides one octahedron.
