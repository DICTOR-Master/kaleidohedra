# Note (DICTO 2026-10-22): widen to all five Platonic solids — study branch only, for later

DICTO's request: extend the swept-solid work from the dodecahedron to all five Platonic solids (tetrahedron, cube,
octahedron, icosahedron as well). Recorded on the research branch only; not part of #49 for now, not started.

What carries over at once: the equilateral-sweep argument is general (a Minkowski sum with a unit segment has edges
that are either the solid's own edges or copies of the segment, merging only when parallel), so any Platonic solid of
edge 1 swept by a unit segment not parallel to an edge is equilateral; faces parallel to the sweep stretch.
