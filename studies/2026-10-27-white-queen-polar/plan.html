The Red Queen 120-cell

Plan: the polar exchange of the White Queen 600-cell

DICTO · idea 27–28 October 2026: "a polar swap of the White Queen", "can we build the polar exchange 120 cell" · study 2026-10-27-white-queen-polar
Goal. Build the 120-cell, the polar of the 600-cell, exactly, and show the exchange between the two: every vertex of one becomes a cell of the other. The White Queen has 600 tetrahedra round 120 corners; her polar, the Red Queen, named after the other queen of Through the Looking-Glass, has 120 dodecahedra round 600 corners. Result wanted: the same proved atlas as #63 for the Red Queen, the exact dictionary between the two, and a Polar button in the White Queen world.

Already proved by our own data

The corners of the Red Queen are exactly the centres of the White Queen's 600 cells. So the Cleo shells of #63 are already a result about the Red Queen:

So the polar exchange of the Cleo core is the dodecahedron itself: twenty Cleo-Icos round a point on one side, twenty corners round a cell on the other. This is the icosahedron–dodecahedron duality of the Platonic solids, one dimension up.

The pentagonal pyramids. Cut from its centre, the Red Queen's core falls into the 12 pentagonal pyramids of the regular dodecahedron, Aristotle's dodecahedron pyramid of #44, as the White Queen's core falls into the 20 Cleo-Icos. The dictionary of the two cores: Cleo-Icos ↔ corner of the dodecahedron (20 ↔ 20); corner of the icosahedron ↔ pentagonal pyramid (12 ↔ 12). In the 120-cell the core is one whole cell; the 12 pyramids are its cut from the centre, not cells of their own.
