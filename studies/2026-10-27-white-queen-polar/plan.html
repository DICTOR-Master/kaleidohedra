Goal. Build the 120-cell, the polar of the 600-cell, exactly, and show the exchange between the two: every vertex of one becomes a cell of the other. The White Queen has 600 tetrahedra round 120 corners; her polar, named here the Red Queen after the other queen of Through the Looking-Glass, has 120 dodecahedra round 600 corners. Result wanted: the same proved atlas as #63 for the Red Queen, the exact dictionary between the two, and a Polar button in the White Queen world.

Already proved by our own data

The corners of the Red Queen are exactly the centres of the White Queen's 600 cells. So the Cleo shells of #63 are already a result about the Red Queen:

The list reads the same backwards (central symmetry)The same, for the same reason

So the polar exchange of the Cleo core is the dodecahedron itself: twenty Cleo-Icos round a point on one side, twenty corners round a cell on the other. This is the icosahedron–dodecahedron duality of the Platonic solids, one dimension up.
