Keyed Penrose seams round one OCD

Picture first. Looking at one OCD (gold: its five-kite stars) with its 12 star-centred W round it, drawn on a sphere just outside the OCD. Black lines: where the W meet. Coloured dots: one W each (its star). Coloured lobes: star-scale thick Penrose rhombi jutting from one W into its neighbour.

  • Exact: round the OCD, every W–W joint passes through the OCD centre, so the 12 W split the space round it like a rhombic dodecahedron — 12 four-sided regions (not five), one star in each; the joints meet 4 at a time (6 places) or 3 at a time (8 places).
  • Every seam carries two lobes, one each way, so each W gives as many lobes as it takes and all 12 stay the same piece. 24 seams, 48 lobes per OCD; lobe edge 0.2166 = the edge of the Penrose rhombus that links facing stars (≈ 8.5 mm at a 60 mm octahedron edge).
  • Only the front half is drawn. In 3D each lobe would run through the joint from the OCD outwards (a tenon), so the pattern is continuous over the whole set.
