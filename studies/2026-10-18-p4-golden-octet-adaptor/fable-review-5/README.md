**Proposal in one paragraph.** Tile = the OCD's star kite (9.4 / 7.8 mm edges at a 60 mm octahedron edge). Tiling = the only edge-to-edge tiling of congruent kites: rows nose to tail, alternate rows pointing the other way, row spacing 4.4 mm. The rows run parallel to the piece's seam edge A0E0 with a row axis on the seam — forced, because the W behind the seam is placed by the half-turn about that edge, which reverses the face normal: the height field must be anti-symmetric across the seam and the kite lines mirror-symmetric about it, and a kite tiling's only mirror lines are its row axes. Heights: row axes alternate 0 (seam), +d/2, −d/2, … so kites are shallow roofs/troughs creased along their long diagonal; d is a field, 2.0 mm at the socket rim fading linearly to 1.1 mm at 37 mm from the OCD centre (depends only on the distance from the nearer OCD centre, hence continuous across every seam and fold). Bump faces A0E0T±, A1E1T±, dent faces A0E1T±, A1E0T± (bipartite pairing); the pattern on one face is carried to the other seven by the piece's 2/m symmetry and the 3-fold turns about the OCD centres — every W identical, every bump meets a dent, exact.

**Measured imprint relief** (for the "as deep as the imprint" end): OCD facets fold by 23.8°; the star centre rises 1.12 mm above its neighbours; a kite roof folding like the OCD over one row is 0.9 mm deep — the imprint's own relief IS about 1.1 mm, so a visible transition needs the rows next to the socket deeper than the imprint (drawn 2.0 → 1.1 mm; alternative 1.1 → 0.5 mm).

**Problems stated on the page:** flat tilted kites are impossible as a periodic tiling (kites must be creased, 10–18°); the five-fold star cannot run into periodic rows (the socket rim is a visible join; one star edge is within 1° of a row direction but aligning it would break the seam mirror); the relief must return to zero along the oblique piece edges A0T, E0T (fade band ~1 row) for a closed solid; the corner E0 cuts the pattern (2.9 kite lengths from A0); the deepest rows sit next to the socket where the OCD shadow overhangs (1.3 mm clearance was needed in the first keyed W).
