## Found — related, none the same
- **Lalvani, US 5,448,868 (1995), "Periodic space structures composed of two nodal polyhedra"** (PDF filed): nodal polyhedra on the vertices of periodic grids (incl. the rhombic-dodecahedron grid) joined by connector polyhedra on the grid edges. **Closest concept** to "solid + glue piece" kits; its nodes/connectors have cubic, tetrahedral or prismatic symmetry, not icosahedral, and no golden envelope or OCD imprints. Cite.
- **Kabai, Wolfram Demonstrations (2007–2008):** "Golden Octet Truss" (one tetrahedral + one octahedral part of a golden rhombohedron, planar and icosahedral lattices) — **a different object with the name "golden octet truss": avoid naming DICTO's kits "golden octet"**. "Relating a Rhombic Triacontahedron and a Rhombic Dodecahedron" (2008) — its text does not say how; **must be viewed (Wolfram player) before claiming "envelope inside the fcc rhombic dodecahedron touching all 12 faces" as new**. "A Rhombic Dodecahedron Made of Rhombic Triacontahedra", "Cluster of 14 Rhombic Triacontahedra" (RTs on an RD's faces/vertices, overlapping in oblate rhombohedra) — RT clusters, different.
- **Koskinen, Bridges 2011** (PDF filed): RTs filling space twice; a concave RT as a single space filler. Different.
- **Lalvani, US 5,623,790** (already checked): non-regular RTs stacked face to face with unnamed gap cells. Different.
- Art practice context: Kijima & Tachi (Bridges 2020) rhombic-dodecahedron block system; Bih-Yaw Jin's octet-truss bead sculptures (Bridges 2017/2018); Fuller's octet truss at MoMA (1959); Kabai's RT cluster sculptures (Bridges 2010).
