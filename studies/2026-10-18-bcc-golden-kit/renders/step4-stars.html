Facing five-fold stars of the golden envelopes (bcc)

Down one five-fold axis. Gold: the five golden rhombi round a five-fold corner of one envelope (RT). Blue: the facing star of the envelope at a third neighbour, pointing back. Both stars lie in G′, the golden gap.

  • Exact: the sideways offset runs along one of corner 1's spokes (and opposite one of its tips).
  • Exact: its length is 2/3 of the tip radius, or 2φ/3 × the spoke (just 8% past the spoke's end).
  • Exact search: it is not φ-scaled spoke or tip vectors, singly or in pairs, so no thick Penrose rhombus joins the two corners the way it did in the octet. The factor 2/3 (the bcc lattice's thirds) is what breaks it.
  • The stars are far apart here (gap 0.6231, against 0.363 in the octet), so a link would be a longer bridge through G′.
