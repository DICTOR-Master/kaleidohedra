# Vacuus, Scutoid Frusta & Aequus: the first two studies (24 November 2026)

DICTO's top-priority project (his page "Vacuus, Scutoid Frusta, & Aequus"; aim: "the exploration classification and possible
combinatory interdependencies & utilization of these three completely unique discoveries"). Classification items 1 and 2 of his page.
Names Vacuus (heart, inherited heart, non-heart), Aequus/Aequi, Scutoid Frusta, amber cell, Fullerene Star: DICTO's.

**Verdict, study 1: all 1232 Aequi classified with exact symmetry checks.** Every centrally symmetric die is a heart (276: Ih 4, Oh 57,
Th 215); every die without central symmetry but with at least one pair of parallel faces is an inherited heart (951: O 31, Td 92, T 828);
**only 5 Aequi are non-heart** (no two faces parallel: Td 2, T 3), all built from one tetrahedron half O8/4a plus halves of the
triacontahedron directions. Only the 4 Ih dice have icosahedral symmetry.

**Verdict, study 2: 15 amber cells (5 Aequi x sc, bcc, fcc moulds at 6/5 s0), all valid exact partitions of the resin.** In 14 of the
15 cells **every scutoid frustum keeps its side count** (no Y vertex anywhere). The one exception: TRT-16 (T 3) in the sc mould, where
**all 16 scutoid frusta change their side count** (12 Y vertices). The Th 60-face die (RD12 + D12 + I12′ + RT24′) gives 60 scutoid frusta
in 4 kinds, all keeping their side count, in all three moulds.

Chiral (two hands): T 831, O 31. Every full group agrees with the group recorded in the picker and in the search studies.

Method (exact):
- The 122 face directions are matched one to one with exact vectors in Q(φ)³ (six base patterns, all permutations and signs).
- Every die is invariant under the frame rotation group T (checked). A finite group of isometries containing this T is one of T, Th, Td, O,
  Oh, I, Ih (I and Ih in two placements), each generated by T and some of: −I, the mirror x↔y, the quarter turn about z, a 5-fold
  rotation of either icosahedron. So the full group is fixed by which of these five maps carry the die to itself. Each map acts on the
  122 directions exactly (exact parallelism in Q(√5)); a face may go only to a face of the same depth. Same orbit: equal depth by
  construction. Different orbits: the 60-digit depths differ by at least 3.1e-5 wherever this was needed.
- One die has two orbits at the same depth to 60 digits: Aequus CIRT-30, T 26 (its C6 and RT24/12a faces both at depth 1). Its
  group was checked both ways (depths equal / unequal): T either way.
- Vacuus kind: heart = centrally symmetric; inherited heart = not centrally symmetric, yet some plate carries a face and its parallel
  opposite face; non-heart = no two faces parallel. Plates = one per line through the centre carrying face normals = N − (parallel pairs):
  heart N/2; non-heart N.
- Icosahedral core: the 4 Ih dice (placement A) share the symmetry of the dodecahedron, icosahedron, icosidodecahedron and Fullerene Star.
  The column parallel_core lists the cores whose faces are all parallel to faces of the die: DI-32 and D·I·RT-62 take all of them,
  DRT-42 the dodecahedron, IRT-50 the icosahedron. Every other die: no icosahedral symmetry; the core sits in a subgroup.

## Study 2: amber cells (A), first set (scripts/aequus_solid.py, amber_aequus.py, run_cells.sh; data/cells; logs/cells)

Amber cell (A): the die as core at every point of a cubic lattice (sc, bcc, fcc), mould = its Voronoi cell at 6/5 s0 (s0 = smallest
scale with the die inside), resin = mould minus die, divided by DICTO's combined method exactly as in study 2026-11-21-amber-cell-sweep
(outer pattern = Voronoi diagram of the face centroids on the mould; Y vertex where the two patterns differ). amber.py is reused
unchanged except: the core is the die; orbits are taken under the frame rotation group T (every Aequus has it); each piece is also
tested for convexity exactly; geometry is saved for the renders.

The die in exact arithmetic: the fair depths are not algebraic, so each die is built from its face planes u·x ≤ D with u exact in
Q(φ)³ and D the rational nearest d·|u| with denominator ≤ 10^15 (d = 60-digit fair depth). All vertices are exact in Q(√5). This
stand-in keeps the frame symmetry exactly, its depths agree with the fair die's to better than 1e-14, and its face lattice is checked
to be the fair die's (face count, corner count, side counts of the faces).

Checks on every cell (all 15 pass): each piece is a closed oriented embedded surface; every wall triangle is used by exactly two pieces
with opposite orientation; every piece has positive exact volume; **piece volumes add to mould − die exactly (gap 0 in Q(√5))**;
winding-number sampling 3729 points, none covered other than once. FINAL ok (logs/run_cells.log).

## What this shows

- Heart, inherited heart and non-heart split the Aequi by symmetry: heart exactly when the group contains −I (Ih, Oh, Th); without −I,
  nearly every die still has parallel face pairs. Non-heart Aequi are rare (5 of 1232).
- A fair die's own face pattern carries over to the mould almost unchanged: with face-centroid seeds the outer pattern had the same
  adjacency as the die in 14 of 15 cells, so the resin divides into scutoid frusta that keep their side count. Side changes need a die
  whose faces are far from the mould's shape (the small tetrahedral TRT-16 in the cube).
