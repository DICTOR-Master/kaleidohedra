a prolate golden rhombohedral lattice), t = 1.390323 smallest without overlap (OG density 0.5633; OGs touch). Space tiled by the prolate golden
rhombohedron of edge t/m (m^3 per period). Skin = bricks meeting an OG minus the OG (one network piece per period, as the OGs touch).
