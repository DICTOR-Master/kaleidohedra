How it was counted. The positions are read from the wizard code (Kaleidohedra repository, master of 12 November 2026), not from screenshots; the script that builds this table also checks that every world of DICTO's gold block has exactly one row (33 worlds + 2 lattices + 2 lattices moved in = 37 rows). The book of each world is the themed book that holds its paper as a chapter (chapter heads of the eleven books, the site's "In the book" labels); Quanta of Space is the home only where no themed book holds it. Two placements are readings, marked: Golden Rhombohedra (Book Nine) and Golamandalas (no book yet).
The one pick is Section 3. Regrouping gives every one of the 37 entries a new number; inside each book today's order is kept. Open points for DICTO are in Section 5.

3. The proposal (one pick)

Inside the gold DICTO block, list the worlds under the five rings in book order: Earth (Books One, Two), Water (Three to Five), Fire (Six, Seven), Wind (Eight), Void (Nine to Eleven). Each ring gets one thin heading in its element colour (green, blue, red, yellow, violet); each world's row carries its book number. DICTO's pieces stay first, above Earth, as now. A ring with no world is not shown. Above the rings, one row of five ring buttons jumps to a ring with one tap, so the newest work (now in Fire) is one tap from the top on a phone. The newest world carries a small "new" mark in its row instead of being moved to the top.

Two worlds that are DICTO's but sit today among the classical lattices of both the Kaleidohedra and the Rhombiverse blocks, Golden Breathing Pyrochlore and Golden Bodhi Pyrochlore Networks, move into the gold block under Book One. Everything else outside the gold block stays where it is (Table 2): the Kaleidohedra and Rhombiverse lattices are all Book Two's classical space-fillers in one block each; Polyhedraverse keeps the Platonic solids at the top of its green list; the dimension tabs stay. The DICTO User Guide's world table and the Almanac follow the new order.

5. For and against, and the rules it meets

For. The apps read as the shelf reads: one work, eleven books, five rings, and a world shows which book explains it (the paper and app pairing rule, made visible). Book Two's worlds (Jewel, Sunstar, the thirteens, the octet networks) sit together, as the book's Part II; the Platonic book's worlds come together (the EKP cell at 26 and Studies at 30 join Every Mix, Reuleaux and Aristotle's Pyramid at 11 to 13). Visitors who know one book find its worlds in one place.

Against, and how the pick meets each rule.

  • New work at the top (DICTO, 8 Oct). Ring order puts the newest worlds, the Bitruncated 600-cell and The 4D Permutohedron (Book Seven), at 28 and 29 instead of 2 and 3, Golamandalas (8 Nov) from 4 to the end, and DICTONIC, placed first by DICTO on 23 Oct, at 16. This is the main cost. The pick answers it with the ring buttons (one tap) and the "new" mark; DICTO may prefer instead to keep a top row of the newest one or two worlds, which would list them twice.
  • One place only (DICTO, 10 Oct). Kept: each world is listed once, under its home book; where a world's papers sit in more than one book (column "also in"), the other books show only as book numbers in its row. The two pyrochlore worlds now appear twice (Kaleidohedra and Rhombiverse blocks); the move ends that.
  • Mobile first. Five headings and one button row add about six rows to a long list; the jump row shortens the scroll to any ring.
  • Nothing unnecessary shown. Empty rings are hidden; Book Eleven has no world yet, so the Void shows Books Nine and Ten only.
  • Polyhedraverse's order (Platonic at the top of the green list, DICTO 9 Oct) is untouched.

Open points for DICTO (one line each): (1) Golamandalas: paper #103 is in no book yet; it is listed last until DICTO names its book. (2) Golden Rhombohedra (the Ammann–Kramer tiles, not DICTO's) is in the gold block today; Book Nine is a reading; it could instead move to the Kaleidohedra block. (3) Targets and Shells have no paper (pairing rule). (4) Generalized Permutohedra stays in the Kaleidohedra block, as not DICTO's mathematics, with a Fire chip for Book Seven.
