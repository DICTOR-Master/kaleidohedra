# Candidate group B: three stated questions on space-filling polyhedra (statements as they would be submitted; none is proved yet unless marked)

## Question T: which rational tetrahedra tile space
- Source: A. A. Chentouf, Y. Sun, "Tetrahedra tiling problem", arXiv:2312.01654 (2023), Theorem 1.2: of the 59 sporadic tetrahedra with all dihedral angles rational multiples of pi (classified by Kedlaya, Kolpakov, Poonen, Rubinstein, "Space vectors forming rational angles", arXiv:2011.14232), "at most 40" tile space; §1.1: "The goal of this paper, not fully attained, is to determine which rational tetrahedra tile space." Background: M. Senechal, "Which tetrahedra fill space?", Math. Mag. 54 (1981) 227-243. "Tile" = congruent copies (mirror images allowed), not necessarily face to face.
- T1 (target). Complete answer: exactly the following m of the 40 tile space (each with an explicit periodic tiling, checked exactly), and the other 40 - m do not (each with a certified obstruction).
- T2 (target, partial). Some of the 40 are already known tilers from the older literature (Sommerville 1923; Goldberg 1974 families) and are identified; a further set is proved not to tile by combining a face-to-face obstruction (edge-length graph parity / angle sums) with a non-face-to-face obstruction; so "at most 40" becomes "at least a and at most b", with the remaining cases listed.

## Question G: rhombic monohedral parallelohedra with every zone of k faces
- Source: B. Grünbaum, "The Bilinski dodecahedron and assorted parallelohedra, zonohedra, monohedra, isozonohedra and otherhedra", Math. Intelligencer 32(4) (2010) 5-15 (author's version 2/3/10, p. 17): "Given an even integer k >= 4, is there a rhombic monohedral parallelohedron such that every zone has exactly k faces? The cube has k = 4, the rhombic dodecahedra K and B have k = 6, and the doubly indented icosahedra D1 and D2 are examples with k = 8. No information is available for any k >= 10." (Parallelohedron = polyhedron, not necessarily convex, homeomorphic to a ball, that tiles space by translates; monohedral = all faces congruent.)
- G1 (target, constructive). There is a non-convex polyhedron with 30 congruent golden rhombic faces (edge directions the six 5-fold axes of the icosahedron), every zone of exactly 10 faces, which tiles space by translations; the tiling is checked exactly in Q(sqrt 5).
- G2 (target, negative partial). No polyhedron of 30 golden rhombi on the six icosahedral directions with every zone of 10 faces tiles by translation (finite exhaustive search, exact).

## Question P: are all space-filling pentahedra known? (the quadrilateral pyramids)
- Source: M. Goldberg, "The space-filling pentahedra II", J. Combin. Theory A 17 (1974) 375-378, §6 (p. 378): "It is still not known whether all the space-filling pentahedra have been found. An analytic method seems to be needed to exhaust the possibilities or to show that no others exist." (First list: Goldberg, JCTA 13 (1972) 437-443.) Known related result: G. Duval, arXiv:2207.01486 (2022): the only right square pyramid with trivial Dehn invariant is the one with apex at the centre of a cube on its face (so the right square pyramids are settled).
- P1 (target). Classification of all pyramids over a square base (apex anywhere) that tile space: exactly the following ones, with tilings; all others have nonzero Dehn invariant or another certified obstruction.
- P2 (target, smaller). Classification of right pyramids over a rectangle (two parameters) that tile space.
