## 1. What the draft establishes

- **Proved.** Sweep counts of simple 4-polytopes: births, swaps, deaths = h1, h2, h3; one contact at a time for d ≥ 5 (#104 Theorems 1, 1′, 2).
- **Proved.** Convex-cell theorem: in a simple space-time complex with convex cells, per volume births = deaths = cells and
  swaps = corners − 2 × cells in every generic direction, so swaps per birth = k/2 − 3 (2026-11-15-sun-granulation; 2026-11-15-tissue-null-models).
- **Proved, for the perfect hexagonal mosaic only.** Compound-eye law: one edge family flips at anisotropy √3 across the rows (2026-11-16-compound-eye-scutoids).
- **Exact value, checked by simulation.** Poisson-Voronoi swaps per birth 24π²/35 − 2 = 4.7677 (simulated 4.7669 ± 0.0006); rates 3.058 (2D), 15.61 (3D) by two methods (tissue-null-models).
- **Computed, consistent.** Toy 3D foams: 30,758 contact changes, none simultaneous (2026-11-15-cosmic-voids); 3D grain-growth model obeys the contact identity (2026-11-15-grains-polycrystals); logger returns the #104 counts exactly (2026-11-15-event-logger).
- **Not confirmed.** Froth 0.245 against the published 0.53; 2D grains; Earth plates; clouds; Sun swap rate (step-zero 0 of 13 settings converge, 2026-11-17-sun-step-zero); Sun net neighbour change (frame-rate free in 16 of 16 settings, but no rung: 2026-11-18-sun-net-change, -net-vs-d, -lineage).
- **Not a test, or parked.** Cosmic voids, interstellar bubbles, sea ice, Jupiter, butterfly eye traces, yew. Granule tubes "0.89 scutoids" fails its shuffled control.
- Of 20 rows: 5 established, 3 consistent, 6 not confirmed, 5 not a test, 1 parked. **No measurement on a natural system is established.**

## 2. Best framing of the scutoid connection

Lead with ONE theorem, the convex-cell theorem, as the space-time form of #104 Theorem 1: *every cell of a moving tiling is a
scutoid tube in space-time, and the number of swaps per cell is fixed by the mean number of walls alone, whichever direction is
called time.* That is the scutoid connection; the swept 4-polytopes and Poisson-Voronoi are its two exact examples.
Cells-to-cosmos is truthful only as **range of application**: the law has no length scale, so it can be asked of any cell
tiling. That part is analogy plus method. Nothing at Earth, Sun or cosmic scale is supported; these belong in a closing "next
rungs" section, each with its own verdict in one line. Measured support today is models only (rungs 1 to 3, and half of rung 4).
