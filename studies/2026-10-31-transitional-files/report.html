3. How each copy was checked

Git. Every file in a copy (outside node_modules, dist, test output, caches) was hashed with git and looked up among all 32,536 objects of the Pi repositories (Kaleidohedra with its worktrees and krp-core submodules, krp-core, Rhombiverse, dictospheres, Polyhedraverse). A copy is SAFE when every file was found, or the only extra files are probes, logs, screenshots, or files shown identical (md5) to a Pi copy.

Zenodo. Zips and PDFs were checked by md5 against the files of their Zenodo records through the public API. Ten source zips were checked; eight match. The real-materials-2 and two-tiles zips are not on their records (only the PDF is), so they are CHECK. All 78 PDFs in the node batch folder sa-batch match a version of their record.

Already done today. Five finished Claude session transcripts (about 800 MB) left the Pi at 11:37 and are now in /mnt/dicto-backup/archive/claude-sessions on the LaCie. Another process is also removing DRAFT PDFs from ~/k-research (they show as deleted, not yet committed). Neither is listed here.

Not transitional, but worth a commit. Several current files in ~/k-research are untracked: papers/4d-shadows/paper.html and papers/series-web-of-bonds/paper.html (sources), the cover scripts' fonts and logos in studies/2026-10-30-quanta-cover/scripts/, and the current PDFs of the HexOct and Quanta books, Star Cores A and C, TI Star, Between the Queens, Face to Face and CW v47. They are on Zenodo but not in git.

Left out. Tools and environments (Python venvs plotenv and geom, llama.cpp, the 92 GB of models, Antiprism's install), Playwright browsers still in use (chromium 1234, 1243 and 1248 are all referenced by installed test packages), logs older than 14 days (1 MB on the Pi, 2.5 MB on the node), memory, the current session, published final PDFs, and the dicto-deploy, dicto-stats, dicto-watchdog and dicto-backup folders. The Kaleidohedra git store on the Pi is 990 MB; git gc may shrink it, but that is not a deletion.
