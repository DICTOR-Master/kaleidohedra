small circle (cap 60 deg here; any cap size keeps every exact condition), level bases -> great circles through two opposite points of
the equator (checked). spherical-yantra.png: from above the cap, and tilted.
cleo_cap.py: the upper great triangle cannot become an exact spherical icosahedron face (its tip starts at 72.53 deg in the flat
perfect yantra and only grows on a sphere; its base angles never reach equality).
cleo_sphere.py (DICTO 11-08 "leaning towards Cleos"): twenty yantras, one in the inscribed circle (20.9052 deg) of each face of the
spherical icosahedron, i.e. on the base of each of the twenty Cleo-Icos; adjacent caps touch; the twelve gaps sit at the icosahedron's
corners. cleo-sphere-yantras.png.
