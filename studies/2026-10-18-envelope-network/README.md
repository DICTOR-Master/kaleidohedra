## What the network is (all from studies/2026-10-18-ocd-octet, steps 21–27 + 3/24; exact in Q(√5))
Rhombic triacontahedra (RT, the "golden envelope") all in the same orientation on the face-centred cubic lattice spanned by R(1,1,0)-type vectors, R = 2φ/3 for the RT with inradius 2/3 (gbpc-geom-exact.json frame). Equivalently: one RT centred in each octahedron of the octet truss of octahedra |x|+|y|+|z| ≤ R and regular tetrahedra.
- RTs never overlap; they fill exactly **90 − 40√5 = 10/φ⁶ = 55.73%** of space (a lattice packing, far below the densest known RT lattice packing ≈ 80.17%).
- Each RT = its part inside its octahedron + exactly 8 three-fold corners poking through the octahedron faces into the tetrahedra; their base corners sit at the **golden-section points (φ : 1)** of the octahedron edges.
- The octahedron corners outside the RT: a **φ⁻²-scaled corner pyramid** (cut off by the RT face plane) + 2 wedges. At every octet vertex the 6 such pyramids and the φ⁻² corners of the 8 tetrahedra form a **φ⁻² cuboctahedron, exactly ⅓ of the RT's volume** (18.58% of space).
- Two-shape kit: RT + (cube of edge R round each tetrahedron centre, minus the RTs) — left and right.
- OCD-specific (not part of this network): the OCD inside the RT, its five-kite stars, middle pieces and Penrose cores.

## Prior-art check (2026-10-18; library first, then web)
**Reference library (~/Documents/kaleidohedra-references) and kaleidohedra docs/SOURCES.md:** nothing on RTs in the octet truss or on this fcc placement. Related entries: Fuller 1961 (octet truss), Lalvani 1990 NASA TM (octahedral–tetrahedral truss part counts), Baer 1970/1973 and Lalvani 1977/1988 (zonohedra, golden rhombi), Kabai 2010/2011 (clusters of RTs), Fujita et al. 2013 (cluster packing in F-type icosahedral alloys).
**Web (added to the library, papers/MANIFEST.json, 4a25278):**
- Betke & Henk 2000, densest lattice packings of 3-polytopes (PDF filed). The densest known RT packing is a lattice packing, density ≈ 0.80174 (arXiv 1206.3716 table, via search summary — to verify). **Our placement (0.5573) is a different, sparser lattice packing**; its interest is the exact octet fit, not density.
- **Lalvani, US 5,623,790 (filed 1994, priority 1987; published 1997): "the rhombic triacontahedron 19a … is packed leaving gaps which can be filled with other cells" (Fig. 34)**, plus two non-convex RTs that fill space alone. The text does not name the arrangement or the gap cells. **Must see Fig. 34 before any originality claim** on an RT packing with filled gaps.
- Lin & Corbett 2006 (Sc–Mg–Zn approximants): triacontahedral clusters packed bcc (1/1) and primitive cubic (2/1) with prolate rhombohedra between — prior art in crystal chemistry for the **bcc** golden-gap work (not for the octet placement).
- Fujita et al. 2013 (already in library): F-type icosahedral approximants as dense packings of Mackay/Bergman clusters on fcc-related sites — related in spirit (icosahedral clusters on fcc), different objects.
**Not found:** any source placing RTs in the octahedra of the octet truss, the 10/φ⁶ fill, the golden-section keys on the octahedron edges, or the φ⁻² vertex cuboctahedron = ⅓ RT.
