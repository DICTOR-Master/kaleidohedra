Stacking ABCACBA, 24 stitches by 43 rows

DICTO · knitting swatch of 31 October 2026
The idea is DICTO's: the knitted fabric is an exact edge-on slice through a stacking of balls, coloured by the pieces between the balls. Chart computed exactly with Claude (Anthropic), generator hexknit.

How to knit every swatch

  • Stockinette. Stitches 1 and the last are edge stitches, worked in the chart colour, not slipped, so the edges show the bias steps.
  • Colour changes: intarsia with small bobbins, twisting at every change; carry a colour along the back only where its next block is 1–2 stitches away.
  • Every bias row keeps the stitch count: one decrease and one increase, each one stitch in from its edge. In the colour runs the increased and decreased stitches are counted as they finish; work each in the colour of its run.
  • Bobble (MB), in the colour of the stitch. RS rows: (k1, yo, k1) into the stitch, turn, p3, turn, k3, turn, p3, turn, sl1-k2tog-psso. WS rows: (p1, yo, p1) into the stitch, turn, k3, turn, p3, turn, k3, turn, p3tog, and push it to the RS; if it sits badly, purl the stitch plain and sew a bobble or French knot there afterwards.
  • Abbreviations: DG dark gold, LG light gold, DB dark blue, LB light blue. Runs are given in working order (RS from the last stitch to stitch 1, WS from stitch 1 to the last).
