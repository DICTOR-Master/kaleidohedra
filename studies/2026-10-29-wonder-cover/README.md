# The WONDER square (book cover)

DICTO's request (2026-10-29): a cover for "Alicia in Wonderland: The Red & White Queens" in the series style ("give the cover a try").

**Conclusion:** first version made, wonder-cover-front.png. Status: shown to DICTO.

- Cyclic Latin square of WONDER (six different letters; QUEENS repeats E).
- W the White Queen (600-cell) and R the Red Queen (120-cell, its 600 corners = the 600-cell's cell centres), each as the outline of its vertex-first shadow; O Alicia (W48), N Alice (W49), D Humpty Dumpty (48 corners), E the White Rabbit (W51): exact slices from DICTO Solids v5 (k-mathdata solids/*.json, decimal corners). Colours: white, gold, blue, teal, lilac, red.
- Title in the series LED letters: ALICIA IN WONDERLAND (gold), THE RED & WHITE QUEENS (RED in red, the rest white), ◆ DICTO ◆ (red).
- scripts/wonder_square.py, scripts/cover_title.py; run on dicto-big-brother.
