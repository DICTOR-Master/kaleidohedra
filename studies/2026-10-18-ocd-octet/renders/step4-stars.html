Two facing five-fold stars in the OCD octet

Looking straight down one five-fold axis. Gold: the five kites round a corner of one OCD. Blue: the five kites round the facing corner of the neighbouring OCD (across the shared octahedron edge), which points straight back. Pale: the next ring of faces of each.

  • Exact: the sideways offset between the two corners equals the star's tip radius (both 0.350487).
  • Exact: the neighbour's corner is not over a gold tip (my earlier guess was wrong); it sits at 180°, where the gold star has an inner corner.
  • The two stars are turned 36° against each other: a half turn, which for a five-fold star is the same as 36°.
  • Gold star surface: inner corners 0.056 and tips 0.128 below its centre; the blue star slopes the other way.
