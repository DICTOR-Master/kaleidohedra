The trimmed middle piece, slice by slice

Slices straight across the octahedron edge two OCDs share (the black dot is the edge). Left and right of the dot: the two octahedra (their caps); above and below: the two tetrahedra on that edge. The red middle piece is what is left after the gold OCDs and purple keys are taken out; where red sits on blue or green it notches that cap or tetrahedron. Symmetric: the slices on the other side of m are these turned half a turn.
