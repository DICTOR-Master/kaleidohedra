A Penrose cut between the two facing stars

Looking down the five-fold axis, as in step 4. Gold and blue: the two facing five-kite stars. Red: one thick Penrose rhombus from corner c1 to corner c2. Purple: two smaller thick Penrose rhombi, each reaching one tip of each star. Proposal by Claude, for DICTO

  • Exact: the line c1 → c2 is the long diagonal of a thick Penrose rhombus (72° at c1 and c2, 108° at the sides), edge 0.2166 = tip radius ÷ φ.
  • Exact: its four edges run along the stars' own spokes, towards a gold tip from c1 and a blue tip from c2.
  • Exact: at each side corner a thick Penrose rhombus one φ-step smaller (edge 0.1339, 108° at the side corner) reaches one gold tip and one blue tip.
  • So three Penrose rhombi in φ-scaled sizes join the two stars, tip to tip, symmetric about the middle point.
