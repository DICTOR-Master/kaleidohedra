# RT centre pyramids and the Dehn test (gap 9), 4 Nov 2026
For a centre pyramid of a face-transitive (isohedral) convex solid, every lateral dihedral is 2π/k (k pyramids round a corner axis) and the base dihedral is half the solid's
dihedral angle θ. So the pyramid's Dehn invariant vanishes iff θ/π is rational (Dehn: Σ length ⊗ angle mod Qπ).
Among the 5 Platonic and 13 Catalan solids (standard dihedral tables), θ/π is rational only for the cube (1/2), the rhombic dodecahedron (2/3) and the rhombic triacontahedron (4/5).
