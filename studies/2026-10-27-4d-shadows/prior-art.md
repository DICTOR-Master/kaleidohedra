## What is already known, and what is not claimed

- The vertex-first shadow of the 600-cell (pentakis icosidodecahedron pattern) is known (Wikipedia). The shadows of the
  5-cell (tetrahedron, triangular bipyramid) and 16-cell (octahedra, hexagonal bipyramid, cube) are elementary.
- The 16-cell edge-first shadow is the cell of the known square bipyramidal honeycomb.
- The dented dodecahedron is the excavated dodecahedron's surface (known deltahedron). The augmented dodecahedron (12
  outward equilateral pentagonal pyramids) and the 12-dented icosahedron were not found in the sources above; they are
  obvious constructions and are not claimed new; they get placeholder names only so the README can refer to them.
- The comparison of 4D shadow and slice face patterns with convex and non-convex deltahedra was not found in any source
  checked.
