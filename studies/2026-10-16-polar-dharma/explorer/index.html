Polar Dharma Explorer

The Spheres of Dharma and their polar image, the cycle flipped up–down. Everything is true geometry, computed live in your browser.

Toggle. The flipped cycle (cool colours) scales by ρ² as you slide. At exactly two settings it clicks into yours (warm): at ρ = φ/2 both run through the same icosahedron and dodecahedron, at ρ = φ√3/2 through the same icosidodecahedron and triacontahedron. The settings differ by exactly ×3 in ρ², not a power of φ, so no toggle shares both.

Infinite core. The outer dodecahedron is exactly φ³ times the inner one, so a copy of the whole nest fills the core, forever, with the great stellated dodecahedron locked at the heart of every turn. Every ×φ³ of zoom brings you back where you started, one turn deeper.

Interlock. Both cycles as one solid: every piece of yours cut by every piece of the flipped cycle, checked exactly in ℚ(√5). Ends shared: 536 pieces, 12 kinds. Middle shared: 502 pieces, 11 kinds. The toggle moves where the two cycles cut each other.

Moving. Drag to turn freely and pinch or scroll to zoom. Play, Fall in, a tab or a view button glides it back to the chosen axis, centred, with up, down, left and right marked.
