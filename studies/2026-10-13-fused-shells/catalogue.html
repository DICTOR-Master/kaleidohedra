Fused Shells Catalogue

Each structure built outwards, and every stage taken as one solid: the pieces fused, only the outside counted. The main route adds one kind of piece at a time; under it, the convex stages of whole rings, then the partial rings that sit on a complete shell. Every other combination was counted too (Spheres of Dharma: 107 of whole rings, 5,569 with partial rings) and is kept as data for the paper. Names marked “lead” are possibilities still to check.
