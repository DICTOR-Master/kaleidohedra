# David and Goliath: prior-art check (2026-10-25)

Names by DICTO. The finding: in A15 (Cr3Si, cube edge 2), the power diagram at λ = w_Si − w_Cr = 3/4 tiles space by Goliath (hull of Jessen's icosahedron, around Si) and David (tetragonal disphenoid, around Cr), 2 + 6 per cube.
Searched: DICTO's library (`ask-library`, INDEX), Wikipedia/Polytope Wiki on Jessen's icosahedron, 5 web searches, Crossref. Reused the #53 relay-census prior-art (`../2026-10-25-relay-census/prior-art.md`) for the λ ≤ 1/4 literature. All DOIs below checked on Crossref on 2026-10-25.

## Exact relation between Goliath and the Z12 polyhedron (computed exactly with Fraction)
- The 12 Cr around Si at the origin sit at (±1/2, 0, ±1) and their cyclic permutations, squared radius 5/4. Goliath's corners are (0, ±1/2, ±1) cyclic, also squared radius 5/4.
- So they are **not equal, and the relation is neither a scaling nor a dual**. Goliath is the Cr Z12 icosahedron **turned 90° about a cube axis**, or equally reflected in the plane x = y. That gives the other pyritohedral setting of the same shape. The two are congruent: both have 6 edges of squared length 1, 24 of squared length 3/2, and volume 7/2.
- Seen as a shift: the Cr Z12 icosahedron of the body-centre Si at (1,1,1), moved to the origin, is **exactly Goliath**. Goliath's corners are the A15 **6d sites**, which are the Cr (6c) sites shifted by (1,1,1). They are the centres of the empty Cr4 tetrahedra.
- Hence **the Goliath + David tiling is the classical A15 cell complex shifted by (1,1,1) (half a body diagonal)**. That complex is the Si-centred Cr12 Z12 icosahedra (each one the union of 20 SiCr3 Delaunay tetrahedra) plus the empty Cr4 tetrahedra around the 6d sites. Per cell, 2·20 + 6 = 46 tetrahedra, which matches the 46 vertices of the Weaire–Phelan cell. David shifted by (1,1,1) is a Cr4 tetrahedron.
- In the same coordinates the A15 Z12 polyhedron is Jessen's convex hull. Cr at (±1/4, 0, 1/2) fractional has the shape (0, ±1, ±2) cyclic, which is Jessen's vertex set.

## Suggested wording
- Do **not** claim that the icosahedron + tetrahedron decomposition of A15 is new. Say it is the classical Frank–Kasper / Delaunay picture of A15, shifted by half a body diagonal, with Goliath congruent to the Si Z12 icosahedron.
- What can be claimed as not found: (i) this tiling arises as the A15 power diagram at λ = 3/4, with the corners landing on the 6d sites; (ii) the plain statement that the A15 Z12 polyhedron and Goliath are both Jessen's convex hull.
- A web search can miss crystallography texts such as Pearson's or Shoemaker's descriptions, and Sinogowitz, Wells and Goldberg were not checked. Phrase it as "not found", not "new".
