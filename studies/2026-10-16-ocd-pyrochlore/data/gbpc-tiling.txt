DICTO · dictospheres.com — Fable, GBPC tiling of G' (exact in Q(sqrt5) unless marked FLOAT)

== A. What G' is (exact)
uncut triacontahedra RT on the bcc sites: 8 neighbours overlap each RT, each overlap volume -704/729 + 320/729√5; triple overlaps of positive volume: 0
union per cell = vol(RT) - (1/2) sum of pair overlaps = -5824/729 + 3040/729√5; K + 24 GBPC per cell = -5824/729 + 3040/729√5; equal: True
K and every GBPC lie inside the uncut RT, and the volumes match, so G' = space minus the union of the uncut RTs (up to measure zero).
Each GBPC is the part of an RT corner cap that sticks out beyond the joint plane and is not inside the joined neighbour.

== B. Walls of G' (exact)
wall components touching the origin RT's faces: 30 (truncated by the finite site set: 0)

== C. Wall test (exact; any arrangement of rhombohedron faces, edge-to-edge or not, one rhombohedron size lam*|v|)

== E. Module check (exact)
M = Z-span of the six five-fold vectors v1..v6 (|v| = icosahedron circumradius): rank 6, its rational span is all of Q(sqrt5)^3, so every exact point lies in (1/N)M for some N. M is closed under multiplication by phi: False
all corners of G' (48 per cell, all lattice translates) lie in one coset x0 + lam*M for lam = phi/(9k) first at k = 1, i.e. lam = phi/9 (edge 0.179782|v|); then also for every lam = phi/(9k), k = 1, 2, 3, ...

== F. Galois count (exact): what the tile frequencies must satisfy in any face-to-face tiling
calibration, U = one RT, A = 0: sigma(vol RT) = -320/27 - 160/27√5; det(A) vol + flux = -320/27 - 160/27√5; identity holds exactly
calibration, U = one RT, A of the bcc lattice: sigma(vol RT) = -320/27 - 160/27√5; det(A) vol + flux = -320/27 - 160/27√5; identity holds exactly
walls of G' per cell: 30 (each inside one RT face: True)
per cell: det(A) vol(G') + wall flux = 704/81 + 928/243√5; sigma(vol G') = 704/81 + 928/243√5; equal: True
equations per cell at lam = phi/9: p*vol(P) + o*vol(O) = vol(G'), p*sigma(vol P) + o*sigma(vol O) = 704/81 + 928/243√5; unique solution p = 66, o = -30 (exact; for lam = phi/(9k) multiply by k^3)
o < 0: NO face-to-face tiling of G' by golden rhombohedra of one size exists, periodic or not, at any scale.

== G. Removing the count obstruction (exact bookkeeping, lam = phi/9; units = oblate volume, x = m + n*phi)
  GBPC: m = 1/6, n = 0
  small oblate (edge lam/phi): m = -3, n = 2
  small prolate (edge lam/phi): m = 2, n = -1
  big oblate (edge phi*lam): m = 1, n = 2
  big prolate (edge phi*lam): m = 2, n = 3
with both sizes (edges phi/9 and 1/9 times |v|) the wall test says nothing (the two periods are incommensurable), and the count needs o - 3o' + 2p' = -30, p + 2o' - p' = 66 (primes = small): solvable with all >= 0, and it forces at least 10 small oblates per cell.
