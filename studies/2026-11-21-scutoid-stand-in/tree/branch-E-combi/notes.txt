Branch E: DICTO's combi method (scutoid + resin), his idea of 21 Nov 2026: "fill the ends in 2D, then cut the resin to fit".
Run: scripts/combi.py LATTICE FACTOR SEEDS (on the compute host; imports C1 and Q(sqrt5) from ../branch-D-resin/scripts/resin.py, unchanged);
scripts/summary.py re-checks every record and prints the table. data/combi_<lattice>_<factor>_<seeds>.json; log.txt (ends FINAL ok).

Setting as branch D: C1 (truncated icosahedron, edge 1, dodecahedron centre pyramid on each pentagon) at the points of a cubic lattice,
mould = Voronoi cell, scale s (s0 = the smallest with C1 inside its cell; at s0 the 12 apices touch the mould). All exact in Q(sqrt5).

Method
1. Two ends. Inner pattern = C1's 80 faces. Outer pattern = 3D Voronoi diagram of 80 seeds (one per face) intersected with the mould
   surface (the cleanest form; for seeds on the mould it equals the Voronoi diagram on the surface). Seeds tried:
   cen = face centroid; cproj = centroid projected from C1's centre onto the mould; nproj = centroid pushed out along the face normal to the mould.
2. Cut. Piece(F) = region between face F and its outer cell. An inner edge F|G whose outer boundary F|G is one chain with matching ends keeps
   its wall (polygon: C1 edge, two rising edges, the chain). Otherwise the vertices are merged into a cluster with one interior vertex m
   (the Y vertex), on the ray from C1's centre through the cluster's mean point, halfway between C1 and the mould: an inner edge with no outer
   partner pinches to m (triangle); an outer boundary with no inner partner grows from m (fan). Non-flat walls are split into flat triangles
   (least-area triangulation, symmetric fan in case of a tie); facet counts below count coplanar triangles as one facet.
3. Checks (each run): every piece surface is a closed oriented 2-manifold; it is embedded (pairwise triangle tests, float64 with 1e-9 margin,
   exact Q(sqrt5) at the margin); every piece has positive exact volume; every wall triangle is used by exactly two pieces with opposite
   orientation. Together these make the pieces a partition of the resin (each piece is the indicator of its inside; the oriented boundaries
   add up to mould minus C1). Volumes add exactly to the resin volume (exact 0 gap). Independent sampling check: winding numbers of all 80
   pieces at 1848-6000 random points of the mould; every resin point covered exactly once, no C1 point covered.
   Scutoid = piece with a Y vertex whose neighbours on the C1 side and on the mould side differ. Kinds = congruence classes (T_h orbit
   members checked congruent). Every dihedral at a C1 edge printed; 157.7612 (lambda*) tested on every dihedral of every piece.

- Centroid seeds give a valid division in all four arrangements. Where the two patterns differ it is always at hexagon-hexagon edges:
  on the mould the two triangles beside such an edge meet across it (a T1 swap); the hexagon-hexagon wall pinches to a Y vertex and the
  triangle-triangle wall grows from it. Every piece touching such an edge is a true scutoid (bcc 6/5 s0: the 8 hexagons on the 3-fold axes
  keep only their 3 triangle neighbours on the mould; every triangle gains 2 neighbours). At bcc s0 only the 6 hexagon-hexagon edges joining
  two hexagons of the 12-orbit swap, so 36 pieces are scutoids and 44 have no Y vertex.
- Central-projection seeds keep all 150 C1 adjacencies and add 72 (one diagonal at each 4-valent C1 vertex, a split 5-valent apex):
  72 scutoid pieces, 60 Y vertices, valid at bcc 6/5 s0 only.
- At the apex pentagons the outer centroid pattern has a 5-valent point on the pyramid axis (5 seeds on a circle), so the apex rises as
  one edge, no Y vertex; at s0 that point is the apex itself (no vertical edge).
- Kinds: 5 in every valid run (the five T_h face orbits; no two orbits congruent). Facets per kind are large where walls bend
  (bcc 6/5 cen: 23, 25, 16, 20, 24; bcc s0 cen: 8, 18, 10, 5, 8).
- Rings at C1: every edge ring closes (it is a tiling). Piece angles at C1 edges include pi/4 + beta (110.9052, unchanged hexagon walls)
  and 2pi/3; the others are not in the basis. 157.7612 occurs nowhere, in any piece, in any valid run.

Best: bcc at 6/5 s0 with centroid seeds: all 80 pieces true scutoids, 5 kinds, exact partition. The tightest true-scutoid division is
bcc s0 with centroid seeds (36 scutoids). The mixed method gives what the pure resin method (branch D, R1) could not: scutoids, in a
valid division.

LEADS
1. Fewer kinds: the outer pattern only has to keep T_h; merging orbits needs seeds chosen so two orbits get congruent pieces.
2. cproj / nproj failures are failures of the one-Y-per-cluster cut, not proofs that no division exists for those patterns (the walls
   could bend differently).
