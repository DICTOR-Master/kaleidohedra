# Harmonic fingerprint of the golden nests and the OG family (2026-11-04) — DICTO: "fingerprint the golden nests and OG networks"
Method: studies/_lib/methods.py (Q3d, W6; calibrated). Data: five-fold-octet study, og-family-solids.json (float corners from the exact builds).
OG family (chain order TQHD, TQP, TQH, TPH, OG, KRE, PHE, TPP, DPHP): all icosahedral, Q4 = Q8 = 0 (calibration).
W6 sign splits the family: corner-type (−, like the icosahedron): TQHD, TQP, OG, KRE; face-type (+, like the dodecahedron): TQH, TPH, PHE, TPP, DPHP.
The sign changes in pairs along the chain, not at every step: it does not reproduce #43 Observation 1 (corner/face alternation on the axes, period 2); it records which of the two icosahedral harmonic shapes dominates.
Lowest Q6 (most "silent" in degree 6): TQP 0.032, PHE 0.039, DPHP 0.037, KRE 0.041. KRE is the quietest overall (Q10 0.010, Q12 0.004).
