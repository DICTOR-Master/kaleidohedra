# LQG bridge (4 Nov 2026; DICTO "Zeptoverse: the smallest scale"): where do flat-space tilers sit in LQG's area space?
An LQG quantum of space is a polyhedron fixed by its face normals and areas (Minkowski; Bianchi–Doná–Speziale 2011). For fixed normals, members
are points of the area space. scripts/bridge.py: per member, area shares by normal class, Dehn zero, local edge-angle count, IQ.
RESULT (necessary conditions; calibrations cube, TO, RD, T(4) minus corners all pass):
- cube ∩ μ·octahedron (199): both tests pass at ONE interior member, the TO (μ = 3/2): square share 1/(1+2√3) ≈ 0.2240 (exact).
- CO ∩ μ·RD (200): none pass both (the edge-angle count never closes); RD at the end calibrates. Dehn zero holds for the whole stretch
  1 ≤ μ ≤ 4/3 (octahedron planes inactive: cube-trimmed RDs, all dihedrals 90/120/135°, rational) and at μ = 5/3 (B3 permutohedron; screen
  tolerance missed it by rounding). CORRECTS the earlier 'zero only at μ = 5/3' (rt-pyramids study, Concordance grid): none tile (135° needs a 90° partner).
- tetrahedron ∩ μ·(−tetrahedron) (199): ONE member, μ = 2 (T(4) minus corners): T-face share 13/14 (exact).
=> In LQG's area space, flat-space tilers are ISOLATED POINTS with exact shares. No physics claim; a geometric statement about the shapes LQG uses.
