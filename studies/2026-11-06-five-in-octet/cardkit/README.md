# The nest as a card kit (DICTO 11-06: "a nested paper card model"; build A, coloured shells that open like Russian dolls)
nest_kit.py (matplotlib/shapely on compute-host) -> nest-kit-A3.pdf (also papers-to-read/Nest-Kit-Five-in-the-Octet-A3.pdf).
Sizes (octahedron edge 100 mm; each inner shell 2% under the exact nest size for the card): octahedron 100.0, icosahedron 52.9,
dodecahedron 28.0, cube 44.4, tetrahedron 61.5 mm. Each net one piece (spanning tree, no overlaps), rotated to fit A3; every cut edge has
exactly one glue tab or (on the lid) a tuck flap - checked (5, 11, 19, 7, 3 cut edges); tabs never overlap a face or another tab.
Build: tetrahedron first; then cube round it, dodecahedron, icosahedron, octahedron, closing each lid over the one inside.
At 150 mm the octahedron and icosahedron nets do not fit A3 (largest compact boxes 450 x 260 and 278 x 356 mm at 130/70 mm edges).
